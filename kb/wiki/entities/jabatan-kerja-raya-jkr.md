# Jabatan Kerja Raya (JKR)

Jabatan Kerja Raya (JKR), the Public Works Department, is a Malaysian government technical agency referenced throughout [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] as an authoritative source and approving body for road and utility infrastructure standards in Selangor.

## Role in the Document

JKR appears as a co-authority alongside the [[entities/road-engineering-association-of-malaysia]] for road design and hierarchy standards. The [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] classification (rural R1–R6 and urban U1–U6 road categories, design speeds, and reserve widths) is sourced from the Road Engineering Association of Malaysia (2002) but is explicitly noted as "subject to updates from the Jabatan Kerja Raya and local authorities (PBT)," positioning JKR as the ongoing technical steward of road design standards even where the original reference document predates it.

JKR is also cited as a reference standard-setter for utility corridor planning: the [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] section (KU1) bases its design requirements for utility corridors on road reserve (R.O.W.) width categories drawn from both PLANMalaysia (2012) and "JKR Selangor 2022" standards, indicating JKR's role in defining minimum road reserve widths and common-trenching provisions for underground utilities.

## Significance

As the state's public works authority, JKR functions as a technical validator and updater of infrastructure design norms that PLANMalaysia Selangor incorporates into planning policy, bridging engineering standards with land-use and development approval processes across Selangor's 12 local authorities.
