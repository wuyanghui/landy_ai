# Second National Housing/Urbanisation Policy (DPN2)

DPN2 is a Malaysian national policy framework referenced across Selangor state planning guidelines as one of the key policy instruments underpinning industrial, housing, business/commercial, infrastructure, community facility, and environmental planning. The abbreviation appears in source documents rendered as **Dasar Perbandaran Negara Kedua** (Second National Urbanisation Policy, 2016–2025) in industrial, business, infrastructure, community facility, and environmental planning contexts and as **Dasar Perumahan Negara 2** (2nd National Housing Policy) in housing planning contexts — both referring to the same tier of national policy guidance that Selangor's technical guidelines align with.

## Role in Industrial Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]], DPN2 is cited under the section on Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan (Development Policy and Policy Framework), alongside other national and state instruments such as [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/nimp2030]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/netr]].

DPN2's objectives and strategies as applied to industrial development include:

- **Affordable housing for industrial workers** — supporting provision of accessible, affordable housing near industrial zones, linking to [[concepts/worker-welfare-facilities]]
- **Efficient and renewable energy use** — promoting energy solutions such as solar farms and biogas within industrial areas, aligning with [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]]

## Role in Housing Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]], DPN2 (2nd National Housing Policy) is one of the national and state policy instruments the housing guideline aligns with, cited alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/drn-2018-2025]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]. In this context, DPN2 sets objectives for adequate, quality, and affordable home ownership and rental housing, with associated strategies and targets covering:

- **Transit-oriented housing** — encouraging housing development integrated with transit infrastructure, aligning with [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]]
- **Rumah Mampu Milik (affordable home ownership)** — supporting the broader affordable housing quota and provision framework described in [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] and implemented through [[entities/rumah-selangorku]]
- **Multi-generational housing** — promoting housing designs that accommodate multiple generations within a household, connecting to [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]]

## Role in Business/Commercial Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], DPN2 (2016–2025) is cited as one of six national and state policy frameworks underpinning Selangor's commercial development guidelines, alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]]. In this context, DPN2 sets objectives and strategies for:

- **Improving urban business competitiveness** — strengthening the positioning of commercial areas within the broader urban economy
- **Upgrading commercial infrastructure and local economic growth** — supporting infrastructure that sustains business activity and local economic development
- **Standardising efficient business planning processes** — promoting consistent, efficient approaches to planning and approving commercial development
- **Strengthening logistics systems and market data** — improving logistics networks and market information supporting business planning
- **Fostering innovation-friendly environments** — encouraging conditions conducive to business innovation

## Role in Infrastructure Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]], DPN2 is cited in the Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan (Development Policy) section of Bahagian A, alongside [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], as a foundational policy shaping infrastructure standards. In this context DPN2 details objectives, strategies, actions, and justifications for:

- **Integrated utility infrastructure** — promoting shared and coordinated utility placement such as Common Utility Tunnels, aligning with [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]]
- **Efficient and sustainable mobility** — supporting electric vehicle charging provision, aligning with [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]], as well as pedestrian and cycling infrastructure to reduce car dependency and improve urban accessibility

## Role in Community Facilities Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], DPN2 is cited in Section 3.5 of the Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan chapter as one of the national and state policy frameworks (alongside [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]) underpinning standards for planning health, education, safety, religious, burial, community, library, open space/recreation, market, and transport terminal facilities. Two guiding principles are highlighted, illustrated with Shah Alam cityscape and OKU (disabled)-friendly infrastructure examples:

- **Prinsip 2: Bandar Berdaya Huni (Livable City)** — promoting livable, well-serviced urban environments as the setting for community facility provision, connecting to [[entities/bandar-berdaya-huni-negeri-selangor]]
- **Prinsip 4: Inclusive & Equitable Urban Development** — ensuring community facilities are accessible and equitably distributed regardless of location or physical ability, aligning with [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]]

This section is paired with the guideline's discussion of [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] (Section 3.6), which builds on DPN2's livability and inclusivity principles to inform safe, well-surveilled facility design.

## Role in Environmental Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]], DPN2 is discussed in Section 3.4 of the Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan chapter, alongside [[entities/dasn]] (Dasar Alam Sekitar Negara) and the [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] state structure plan, as part of the national and state policy basis for environmental planning. In this context, DPN2's 2016–2025 urban development objectives are read for their environmental implications:

- **Objektif 2.10: Comprehensive disaster risk management** — supporting geological and slope stability assessment ahead of development, aligning with the guideline's disaster risk reduction principles for hillside, highland, and landslide-prone areas
- **Objektif 5.2: Efficient and sustainable energy use** — echoing the guideline's broader push toward green building and resource-efficient development
- **Objektif 5.4: Efficient management of water bodies** — supporting conservation and buffer standards for rivers and lakes, aligning with [[concepts/river-reserve-development]]
- **Objektif 5.6: Increasing the size and quality of green areas** — reinforcing the guideline's emphasis on preserving natural topography, tree cover, and ecologically sensitive landscapes

## Relevance

DPN2 provides the national policy basis for balancing development growth — whether industrial, residential, commercial, infrastructural, community-facility-related, or environmental — with liveability and competitiveness considerations, spanning worker housing conditions, sustainable energy practices, affordable home ownership, multi-generational living, urban business competitiveness, integrated sustainable infrastructure provision, livable and inclusive community facilities, and disaster risk reduction, water body management, and green area conservation. Its objectives are further detailed and operationalised through implementing instruments like [[entities/act-446]], [[entities/strata-titles-act-1985]], [[entities/dasn]], and standards developed by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
