# Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB)

Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) is Malaysia's national electricity utility, responsible for power generation, transmission, and distribution. In the [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] guidelines, TNB is the governing authority whose technical standards underpin the electrical infrastructure sections of Selangor's infrastructure planning framework.

## Role in the Document

TNB's authority is invoked throughout the electrical infrastructure guidance (PE1 and PE2):

- **Substation siting ([[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]])** — Standards for site and building area requirements across substation types (Pencawang Masuk Utama/PMU, Pencawang Pembahagian Utama/PPU, Stesen Suis Utama/SSU, Pencawang Elektrik/PE), spanning voltage classes from 11kV to 132/33/11kV and AIS/GIS configurations, must comply with TNB's own "Piawaian Rekabentuk Pencawang Elektrik" (Electrical Substation Design Standards). The document notes that final decisions on site or building size changes remain subject to TNB approval.
- **Transmission line corridors (Rentis TNB)** — TNB sets reservation requirements for transmission towers and lines, including a minimum 30.48m (100ft) setback between pylon legs and the road/land reserve boundary, and forest/other-land reserve widths of 20m per side for 33kV/132kV/275kV lines and 25m per side for 500kV lines. No buildings or pipelines are permitted within these corridors.

## Relationship to Other Standards

TNB's siting rules intersect with other infrastructure requirements in the guidelines, such as minimum setback distances from sewerage treatment plants and flood-prone area platform levels for substations, and coordination with [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] governing shared utility placement along road reserves. As with [[entities/jabatan-kerja-raya-jkr]] for road standards, TNB functions as the sectoral technical authority whose specifications the guideline incorporates by reference rather than restates independently, alongside the coordinating role of [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] in compiling the overall document.
