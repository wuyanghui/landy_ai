# Jabatan Agama Islam Selangor (JAIS)

Jabatan Agama Islam Selangor (JAIS) is the Selangor state Islamic religious authority referenced in the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline as the agency responsible for administering Islamic religious facilities within the state.

## Role in Community Facility Planning

Under the KM4-2 (Surau) standards, completed surau sites developed per the guideline's planning criteria are required to be handed over to JAIS upon completion. This positions JAIS as the receiving authority for surau facilities once they meet the population catchment, site area, and building component standards set out in the guideline (minimum 0.20 hectare site, prayer hall, ablution areas, funeral management room, and related facilities).

The guideline's KM4 chapter (Kemudahan Keagamaan) covers three tiers of religious facilities under a broadly parallel planning framework: mosques (KM4-1, ranging from Masjid Negeri down to local/kariah-level, minimum 3–10 acres depending on tier), suraus (KM4-2, minimum 0.20 ha, serving 500 units/2,000 residents), and non-Islamic places of worship (KM4-3, minimum 1.50 acres/0.61 ha serving 5,000 residents, covering Buddhist, Christian, Hindu, Sikh, and Tao communities). JAIS's explicit administrative role is noted specifically in relation to surau handover, distinguishing its function from the broader siting standards that apply across all three facility types.

## Related Standards and Bodies

- Surau siting must follow orientation toward the qiblat and setback requirements from electrical substations and transmission lines, subject to [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] verification from [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]] on Environmentally Sensitive Areas (KSAS) Class III and IV land.
- Mosque (KM4-1) facilities follow a similar siting logic — population catchment, qiblat orientation, road access, and buffer requirements from noise/vibration sources — with building components scaled by tier (state, district, local), including prayer halls, imam's offices, zakat collection offices, and funeral management rooms.
- Non-Islamic place of worship (KM4-3) planning follows parallel but distinct siting and design standards (e.g., height restrictions, skyline harmony, neutral prayer/meditation room provisions in stratified housing), though JAIS's role is specifically noted in relation to surau handover rather than these facilities.
- As a state religious authority, JAIS operates alongside other Selangor state bodies referenced in the guideline, such as [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]], which issued the overall guideline.
