## Overview

PLANMalaysia Selangor (Jabatan Perancangan Bandar dan Desa Negeri Selangor) is the state-level town and country planning department responsible for producing statutory and technical planning guidance for Selangor. It is the publisher of the [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025"; the [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025"; the [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor, 2025"; the [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor, 2025"; the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025"; and the [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] guideline, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor, 2025."

## Role in the Industrial Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor authored and issued the industrial guideline in collaboration with 12 local authorities (including MBSA, MBPJ, MBSJ, and MDSB, among others) and professional/industry associations (PAM, LPBM, MIP, REHDA). The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction and general planning standards for industrial land use
- Aligning industrial planning with national policy instruments such as [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/nimp2030]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/netr]]
- Setting type-specific technical standards for the seven industrial typologies covered in Bahagian B of the guideline (terrace, detached/cluster, strata, stratified, data centres, worker housing, and Managed Industrial Parks)
- Coordinating with local authorities (PBT) and other agencies (e.g., JAS for environmental review) on approval and implementation of industrial development standards

## Role in the Housing Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor prepared the 2025 housing guideline jointly with 12 Selangor local authorities and the same professional bodies (LPBM, PAM, IEM, MIP, REHDA), superseding the 2022 manual following approval at MMKN meetings Ke-33/2025 and Ke-34/2025. The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction and general planning standards for housing development, uniform across PBT and developers
- Aligning housing planning with national and state policy instruments such as [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/drn-2018-2025]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]
- Embedding the state's [[entities/bandar-berdaya-huni-negeri-selangor]] livability framework and [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] principles into housing planning
- Administering the [[entities/rumah-selangorku]] 3.0 affordable housing policy, including zoning tiers and affordable-housing quota tables
- Setting type-specific technical standards for the seven housing typologies covered in Bahagian B of the guideline (terrace, semi-detached/cluster, detached, landed strata, strata cluster, condominium, and Rumah Selangorku)

## Role in the Business Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor prepared the 2025 business/commercial planning guideline jointly with local authorities across Selangor (including MBSA, MBPJ, MBSJ, MBDK, MPAJ, MPS, MPKlang, MPKS, MPHS, MDSB, and MPKajang) and professional bodies (JPBD, LPBM, MIP, PAM, REHDA), superseding the fourth edition (2022) following approval at MMKN meetings in November 2025. The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction and general planning standards for commercial and business development, aimed at supporting economic growth, competitiveness, and sustainability
- Aligning business planning with national and state policy instruments such as the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]]
- Setting type-specific technical standards for the twenty commercial typologies (PN1–PN20) covered in Bahagian B of the guideline, ranging from shophouses and hypermarkets to hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, golf courses, and sports complexes
- Defining general site-planning principles (Section 5.1) covering statutory-plan compliance, hierarchical development intensity, business diversification, accessibility, complete supporting infrastructure, safety, and design innovation
- Coordinating with [[entities/jabatan-bomba-dan-penyelamat]] and referencing [[entities/ms1184]] for fire-safety and OKU accessibility compliance across all commercial development types

## Role in the Infrastructure Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor prepared the 2025 infrastructure guideline jointly with Selangor's 12 local authorities (Shah Alam, Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Diraja Klang, Ampang Jaya, Kajang, Selayang, Sepang, Kuala Langat, Kuala Selangor, Hulu Selangor, and Sabak Bernam), following endorsement at MMKN meetings in 2022 and 2025, and positions the document as supporting the Smart and Caring Selangor agenda. The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction and technical standards for infrastructure and utility development, covering transportation and traffic, electrical substations and transmission corridors, telecommunications towers, utility corridors, and sewerage treatment plants
- Aligning infrastructure planning with national and state policy instruments such as the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]
- Setting [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] standards distinguishing rural (R1–R6) and urban (U1–U6) road classes, sourced in part from the [[entities/road-engineering-association-of-malaysia]] and subject to updates from [[entities/jabatan-kerja-raya-jkr]]
- Defining detailed [[concepts/parking-standards]] for cars, motorcycles, buses, lorries, bicycles, OKU users, and women, alongside [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] for EVCB siting, sizing, and fire-safety separation
- Setting [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]] standards for TNB substations (PMU, PPU, SSU) and transmission corridor (rentis) setbacks, in coordination with [[entities/tenaga-nasional-berhad-tnb]]
- Establishing telecommunications tower siting standards (spacing, setbacks, camouflage, co-location) and periodic inspection requirements coordinated with PBT and SKMM
- Setting [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] for shared trenching of electrical, communication, water, sewerage, and gas lines within road reserves, including open-space and affordable-housing incentives for corridor land surrender
- Defining sewage treatment plant siting criteria and land-area standards by population-equivalent class, referencing the Malaysian Sewerage Industry Guidelines (MSIG)

## Role in the Community Facilities Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor prepared the 2025 community facilities guideline (a revision of the 2022 MGPPPNS Edition 4) jointly with 12 Selangor local authorities (MBSA, MBPJ, MBSJ, MBDK, MPAJ, MPKajang, MPSelayang, MPSepang, MPKuala Langat, MPKS, MPHulu Selangor, MDSabak Bernam) and professional bodies (LPBM, PAM, MIP, REHDA), following approval at MMKN meetings 33/2025 and 34/2025 (November 2025). The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction and general planning standards for ten categories of community facilities: health, education, safety, religious, community, burial, public libraries, open space/recreation, public markets, and public transport terminals
- Aligning community facility planning with national and state policy instruments such as the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dpn2]]
- Embedding [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] and [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] principles into site selection and facility tiering across all nine specific guideline chapters (KM1–KM9)
- Requiring [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] verification from [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]] for sites in environmentally sensitive or geologically unstable zones, applied consistently across health, education, safety, religious, and burial facility standards
- Setting horizontal and [[concepts/vertical-facility-development]] design criteria for land-constrained urban sites, including compact high-rise schools, hospitals, and community complexes
- Coordinating with [[entities/kementerian-kesihatan-malaysia]] on hospital and health clinic siting standards, with [[entities/polis-diraja-malaysia]] and [[entities/jabatan-bomba-dan-penyelamat]] on police and fire station standards, with [[entities/jabatan-agama-islam-selangor-jais]] on mosque and surau handover, and with [[entities/jabatan-kebajikan-masyarakat]] on children's, elderly, and community rehabilitation activity centres (PAKK/PAWE/PPDK)
- Administering the [[entities/tabung-amanah-perkuburan-negeri-selangor]] developer contribution mechanism for burial land provision in large housing projects
- Applying consistent [[concepts/parking-standards]], [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], and [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] across all nine facility categories, and governing strata-sited facilities (e.g., tadika/taska, community complexes) under the [[entities/strata-titles-act-1985]]

## Role in the Environmental Planning Guideline

PLANMalaysia Selangor prepared the 2025 environmental planning guideline jointly with 12 Selangor local authorities (Shah Alam, Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Klang, Ampang Jaya, Kajang, Selayang, Sepang, Kuala Langat, Kuala Selangor, Hulu Selangor, and Sabak Bernam) and professional bodies (LPBM, PAM, IEM, PIAM, REHDA), superseding the 2022 Environmental Planning Manual following approval at MMKN Ke-33/2025 (7 November 2025) and endorsement at MMKN Ke-34/2025 (12 November 2025). The department is responsible for:

- Establishing state-wide policy direction to help PBT and Selangor state agencies manage the environmental impact of development based on [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] criteria identified within Structure Plans, Local Plans, and Special Area Plans (RT/RKK)
- Aligning environmental planning with national and state policy instruments such as the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/dasn]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]
- Setting basic planning principles covering disaster risk reduction, ecological sensitivity preservation, scenic value, water catchment function, and cultural/historical landscape identity that underpin all technical standards in the guideline
- Setting hillside and highland development standards (slope classification Kelas I–IV cross-referenced against elevation bands) and peat/soft-soil area controls, requiring geotechnical and geological reporting from registered consultants appointed via [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]]
- Establishing natural topography and lake-area preservation zoning (littoral zone, ecozone, catchment zone) for natural and man-made lakes, and tree preservation order standards (measurement criteria, prohibited activities, and penalties) under [[entities/akta-172]]
- Setting coastal/beachfront development setback distances by beach type and land use, and [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] standards covering reserve width determination, permitted/prohibited waterfront structures, and riverbank protection methods, in coordination with [[entities/jabatan-pengairan-dan-saliran-jps]] and [[entities/lembaga-urus-air-selangor-luas]]
- Mandating [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]] system design standards for buildings with roof catchment area of 100m² or more, covering gravity-fed, direct-pump, and indirect-pump installation methods and landscape-based detention/retention components
- Coordinating with [[entities/jabatan-alam-sekitar-jas]] and other technical agencies on enforcement and approval of environmentally sensitive development

## Related Themes

As the issuing authority for all six guidelines, PLANMalaysia Selangor embeds [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]] principles (Eco-Industrial Park practices), [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]] between industrial, religious, burial, and residential land uses, and [[concepts/worker-welfare-facilities]] standards referencing [[entities/act-446]] in its industrial guideline. In its housing guideline, it embeds [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] and [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]] (OKU, elderly, and childcare provisions), and governs strata housing schemes under the [[entities/strata-titles-act-1985]]. In its environmental guideline, it embeds [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] criteria, [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] standards, and [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]] requirements as recurring safeguards across hillside, lake, coastal, and river-reserve development. Across the industrial, housing, business, infrastructure, community facilities, and environmental guidelines alike, it applies consistent [[concepts/parking-standards]], [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]], and [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] to every development typology it regulates, embeds [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] as a recurring safety-by-design principle, and grounds its technical standards in the same recurring national/state policy stack — [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]].
