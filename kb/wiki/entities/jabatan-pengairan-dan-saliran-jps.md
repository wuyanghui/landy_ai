# Jabatan Pengairan dan Saliran (JPS)

Jabatan Pengairan dan Saliran (JPS), the Department of Irrigation and Drainage, is a federal technical agency responsible for river management, drainage, and flood mitigation standards referenced throughout [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]].

## Role in the Document

JPS is cited as one of the primary technical authorities underpinning [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] (AS5), particularly in setting river reserve width tables by river width category and defining determination methods for natural, lined, embanked, and semi-natural channel cross-sections. Its guidelines are listed among the 29 legal/regulatory references (Punca Kuasa / Peruntukan Perundangan) that form the legal foundation of the guideline document.

JPS is also named alongside [[entities/lembaga-urus-air-selangor-luas]], PBT, and PBN as one of the authorities requiring coordination and approval for development within or adjacent to river reserves and control zones.

## Related Standards

- River reserve width and setback determination under [[concepts/river-reserve-development]]
- Drainage and stormwater management (MSMA) requirements referenced in hillside development reporting under [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]]
- Coordination on rainwater harvesting and landscape-based detention components under [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]]
