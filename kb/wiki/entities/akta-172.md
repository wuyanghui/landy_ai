# Akta Perancangan Bandar dan Desa 1976 (Akta 172)

Akta Perancangan Bandar dan Desa 1976 (Akta 172) — the Town and Country Planning Act 1976 — is the principal federal legislation governing land use and development planning in Peninsular Malaysia, including Selangor. It is cited as one of the foundational legal instruments underpinning both the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline and the [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] guideline.

## Role in These Documents

Akta 172 is listed among the legal basis and legislative provisions (Punca Kuasa/Peruntukan Perundangan) that give authority to the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025" and the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor 2025." It provides the statutory planning framework within which local authorities (PBT) and the state (PBN) prepare structure plans, local plans, and specific guidelines such as these.

## Specific Application: Open Space Provision

Within the community facilities guideline, Akta 172 is directly invoked in **KM7 (Tanah Lapang dan Rekreasi)**, which defines open space requirements under the Act — including the 10% open space provision for landed and stratified housing developments, with 70% designated for active recreation. This statutory basis extends to the hierarchy of open space types (Taman Wilayah/Taman Negeri, Taman Bandar, Taman Tempatan, Padang Kejiranan, Padang Permainan, Lot Permainan) detailed in that guideline.

## Specific Application: Tree Preservation Order (AS3)

Within the environmental planning guideline, Akta 172 is the statutory basis for **AS3: Perintah Pemeliharaan Pokok Mengikut Peruntukan Akta 172**. Sub-section 35A(1) (Bahagian VA of the Act) empowers local planning authorities (PBPT) to gazette Tree Preservation Orders — implemented via the Kaedah-Kaedah Perintah Pemeliharaan Pokok 2001 — to safeguard trees of intrinsic, historical, ceremonial, or conservation value, as well as rare, endemic, and endangered species. The Act sets measurement criteria for protected trees (girth exceeding 0.8 metres, measured at 1.3 metres from ground level, with exceptions for flood-affected trees), prohibits felling, cutting, pruning, uprooting, or damaging protected trees without authorisation, and prescribes penalties under sub-section 35H(1)/(3) of up to RM10,000 or three months' imprisonment, or both, for offences. Enforcement follows Kaedah 6(3) of the Perintah Pemeliharaan Pokok 2001.

Akta 172 is further referenced in the guideline's hillside and highland development standards (AS1-1), where topography preservation requirements for hill and highland areas draw on the Act's planning-control provisions alongside [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] criteria.

## Relationship to Other Frameworks

Akta 172 operates alongside other statutory and policy instruments referenced in these guidelines, including [[entities/strata-titles-act-1985]] (for stratified developments) and state-level policy documents such as [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] and [[entities/dpn2]], which give planning direction within the statutory bounds set by Akta 172. It also frames guidance on [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] and site standards issued by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
