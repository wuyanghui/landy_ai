# Malaysia Road Safety Plan (PKJR) 2022–2030

The Malaysia Road Safety Plan (Pelan Keselamatan Jalan Raya, PKJR) 2022–2030 is a national policy instrument aimed at reducing road traffic fatalities and injuries through improved road design, enforcement, and infrastructure standards. It is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] as one of the policy foundations underpinning Selangor's infrastructure planning guidelines.

## Role in the Document

PKJR is listed alongside other national and state policy instruments — including [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] — as part of the policy and planning basis (Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) set out in Bahagian A of the guideline. Its safety-focused goals directly inform the document's technical standards on:

- [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] — design speed, access control, and geometric standards across road classes
- Pedestrian and accessibility infrastructure design, including CPTED-based safety measures (lighting, CCTV, sightlines, safe crossing points) and Universal Design standards referenced under [[entities/ms1184]]
- [[concepts/parking-standards]] — safety and lighting requirements for vehicle parking areas

## Significance

As a national road safety framework, PKJR 2022–2030 provides the safety rationale behind many of the detailed engineering and layout requirements found in the Pengangkutan dan Lalu Lintas (PL) section of the guideline, reinforcing the document's broader goal of ensuring orderly, safe infrastructure development across Selangor, consistent with the aims of [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
