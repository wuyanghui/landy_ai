# MS1184

MS1184 is the Malaysian Standard that sets out design and construction requirements for accessibility to and within buildings for persons with disabilities (Orang Kurang Upaya / OKU). It is the technical reference cited throughout [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]], and [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] for ensuring that commercial developments, public infrastructure, and community facilities in Selangor provide adequate access, facilities, and amenities for disabled and elderly users.

## Role in PEQ-Perniagaan

MS1184 compliance is invoked as a mandatory requirement across nearly all twenty specific planning guideline chapters (PN1–PN20) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor 2025," published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]]. It governs:

- **OKU parking bays** — typically a minimum 2% quota of total car parking, positioned near lift access with ramps and railings, as part of broader [[concepts/parking-standards]].
- **OKU-accessible facilities** — ramps, railings, walkways, and toilet provisions required in developments ranging from terrace shops and shopping complexes to hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, golf courses, and sports complexes, forming a core element of [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]].
- **Electric vehicle charging bays (EVCB)** — several chapters specify EVCB units designed with OKU-friendly access, linking MS1184 compliance to [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]].
- **Fire safety coordination** — OKU accessibility provisions in the document are frequently listed alongside firefighting apparatus requirements under [[entities/jabatan-bomba-dan-penyelamat]] standards, reflecting integrated safety and accessibility design.

## Role in PEQ-infrastruktur

MS1184:2014 is also the governing reference for accessibility and universal design provisions in the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor 2025." Specific applications include:

- **OKU car parking bays (TLK5)** — signage design and vertical signboard dimensions for designated OKU parking bays (Rajah TLK5(1)) are drawn directly from SIRIM MS 1184:2014, alongside bay sizing (5.40m x 3.60m with 1.20m transfer area), wheelchair symbol marking, and step-ramp connections, all under [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] and [[concepts/parking-standards]].
- **Universal Design (UD) standards for pedestrian infrastructure (PL4)** — main entrance access ramps (gradient ≤1:20), pedestrian walkway widths, tactile guidance and warning strips, and kerb ramp specifications reference Universal Design principles aligned with MS1184.
- **Resting areas and street furniture (Rajah PL4(11))** — bench and resting-area dimensions along pedestrian walkways are specified with direct reference to MS1184:2014, ensuring rest points are usable by elderly and disabled pedestrians.

## Role in PEQ-K-Masyarakat

MS1184 and Universal Design principles underpin the accessibility provisions woven throughout the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025" across all nine community facility categories (KM1–KM9):

- **Cross-cutting general standard** — Section 6.0 (Piawaian Umum) establishes universal design and OKU accessibility as a baseline requirement applying to all community facility types unless overridden by facility-specific standards, and Section 5.0 (Prinsip Asas Perancangan) names Universal Design Guidelines and OKU-inclusive pedestrian/cycling connectivity as a core planning principle under [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]].
- **Consistent OKU parking quotas** — a recurring 2% OKU parking bay allocation (or comparable ratios) is specified across nearly every facility type, including hospitals and clinics (KM1), schools (KM2), police and fire stations (KM3), mosques, suraus, and non-Islamic places of worship (KM4), cemeteries (KM5), community centres and libraries (KM6–KM7), public markets (KM8), and transport terminals (KM9), reinforcing [[concepts/parking-standards]].
- **Policy alignment** — Section 3.5 (DPN2) illustrates OKU-friendly infrastructure as an example of [[entities/dpn2]]'s inclusive and equitable urban development principle, tying MS1184-based design directly to state urban policy.
- **Facility-level accessibility features** — beyond parking, guidelines call for OKU-accessible walkways, ramps, and lift provisions (e.g., lifts required in special-needs schools exceeding two storeys under KM2, and OKU referral/registration integration in community rehabilitation centres (PPDK) under KM6), extending MS1184's reach beyond parking into circulation and building design.

## Significance

As a recurring cross-cutting technical reference, MS1184 functions as the baseline compliance standard that unifies OKU and universal-design accessibility requirements across commercial development, public infrastructure, and community facility planning in Selangor — spanning building access, parking, pedestrian walkways, street furniture, EV charging facilities, and facility circulation — rather than being restated in full detail in each guideline chapter.
