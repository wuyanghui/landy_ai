# Bandar Berdaya Huni Negeri Selangor

**Bandar Berdaya Huni Negeri Selangor** (Selangor Livable City) is a state policy framework referenced in the [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] guidelines as one of the foundational policies shaping housing planning standards in Selangor.

## Framework Structure

The policy is built on four pillars:

- **Growth** — supporting economic and population expansion in tandem with housing supply
- **Livability** — ensuring residential environments are comfortable, safe, and conducive to daily life
- **Sustainability** — embedding environmental and resource considerations into urban and housing development
- **Resiliency** — building capacity for communities and infrastructure to withstand disruption

## Role in Housing Planning

Within the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025* ([[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]]), Bandar Berdaya Huni Negeri Selangor is cited alongside other state and national policies — including [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] and [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] — as part of the policy stack that informs the guideline's objectives and standards. Its four pillars echo throughout the document's broader emphasis on inclusive, sustainable, and well-integrated residential development, including initiatives tied to [[entities/rumah-selangorku]] affordable housing.

## Publisher

The framework is administered in connection with [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]], the state department responsible for town and country planning in Selangor.
