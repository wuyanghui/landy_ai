## Overview

The Workers' Minimum Standards of Housing and Amenities Act 1990 (Act 446) is a Malaysian federal statute establishing minimum requirements for housing and amenities provided to workers, particularly in industrial and estate settings. Within the [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] document, published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]], Act 446 is cited as the governing legal basis for worker dormitory (Asrama Pekerja) provisions in industrial developments.

## Role in the Guideline

Act 446 underpins the general standards for worker accommodation set out in Bahagian A of the guideline, including:

- Building setback requirements for worker dormitories within industrial lots
- Size limits — dormitory floor area capped at a maximum of 15% of the plot's total gross floor area (GFA)
- Safety and segregation rules for on-site worker housing

These requirements feed into the guideline's broader [[concepts/worker-welfare-facilities]] framework, which spans general industrial standards as well as the dedicated Centralised Labour Quarters (CLQ) typology (IND6) for developments with 500 or more workers, itself further governed by the Housing and Centralised Labour Quarters Standards Regulations 2020.

## Significance

By anchoring dormitory and amenity standards to a binding federal act rather than discretionary guidance, the document ensures that industrial developers in Selangor meet statutory minimums for worker housing quality, safety, and welfare alongside the state's own planning and design controls.
