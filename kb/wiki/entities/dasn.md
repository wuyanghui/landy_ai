# Dasar Alam Sekitar Negara (DASN)

Dasar Alam Sekitar Negara (National Policy on the Environment) is a Malaysian national policy that sets out principles for harmonising economic development with environmental conservation. It is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] as one of the national policy frameworks underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor, 2025*, published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].

## Eight Principles of Sustainable Development

DASN sets out eight principles for reconciling economic growth with environmental protection, including:
- Sustainable use of natural resources
- Conservation of biological diversity
- Environmental stewardship and monitoring
- Active participation in the international community on environmental matters

## Strategi Hijau Malaysia (Malaysian Green Strategy)

Associated with DASN is the Strategi Hijau Malaysia, which focuses on four areas:
- Natural resource management
- Education and awareness
- Pollution control
- Strengthening of institutions

## Six Focus Areas (DASN 2002)

The 2002 iteration of DASN identifies six key areas of attention:
1. Education and awareness
2. Management of natural resources and the environment
3. Integrated development planning and implementation
4. Prevention and control of pollution
5. Strengthening of administrative and institutional mechanisms
6. Proactive approach to regional and global environmental issues

## Role in the Selangor Guideline

DASN is presented alongside [[entities/dpn2]] (Second National Urbanisation Policy) and the [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] (Selangor State Structure Plan 2035) as part of the national and state policy hierarchy that frames the guideline's technical standards, including [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] designation, coastal management, and integrated river basin management.
