Polis Diraja Malaysia (PDRM), the Royal Malaysia Police, is the national law enforcement agency of Malaysia. In [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], PDRM is the operating agency underpinning the **KM3: Kemudahan Keselamatan** (Safety Facilities) chapter, which sets planning and site standards for the police facility hierarchy it operates across Selangor.

## Facility Hierarchy

The guideline defines four tiers of PDRM facilities under [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]], each with escalating site area and service catchment requirements:

- **Ibu Pejabat Polis Kontijen (IPK)** — State-level Police Contingent Headquarters, the highest-tier facility per state, minimum site area 15 acres (6.07 ha), sited within 5km of city centres.
- **Ibu Pejabat Polis Daerah (IPD)** — District Police Headquarters, second-tier facility per district, minimum site area 10 acres (4.05 ha).
- **Balai Polis** — Police Stations, serving a catchment population of 15,000–40,000, minimum site area 5–10 acres (2.02–4.05 ha).
- **Pondok Polis** — Police Beat Bases, serving 5,000–10,000 residents, minimum site area 2.5 acres (1.01 ha).

## Siting and Design Standards

Standards for all PDRM facility tiers specify: visibility and accessibility from main roads; proximity to administrative/institutional centres; minimum distances from projected urban growth zones (10–20 years) and fire/high-risk cargo areas; avoidance of environmentally sensitive areas and unstable geology, verified through [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] from [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]]; minimum building setbacks (20m from main roads); and road widths following [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] (internal roads minimum 15.24m, service roads minimum 20.12m). Facilities also carry defined [[concepts/parking-standards]] covering staff, motorcycle, [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], and [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] provisions.

Building components across the hierarchy include administrative offices, staff quarters, canteens, prayer rooms, storage, parade grounds, guard posts, and fire-fighting equipment stores. The guideline references source standards from JPM (2015) and GPP Kemudahan Masyarakat (2022), and advises developers to consult PDRM and related agencies early in site planning. Illustrative examples cited include IPK Selangor, IPD Shah Alam, and Pondok Polis Taman Sri Manja.
