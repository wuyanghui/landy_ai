# Dasar Rumah Negara (DRN) 2018–2025

The **Dasar Rumah Negara (DRN) 2018–2025** (National Housing Policy) is a federal-level policy framework that shapes housing development strategy across Malaysia, including Selangor. It is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] as one of the key national and state policies with which the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025* (GPPP 2025) aligns.

## Strategic Thrusts

DRN sets out five strategic thrusts guiding national housing policy:

1. **Quality** — ensuring housing meets adequate standards of construction and livability.
2. **Accessibility** — improving access to housing across income groups.
3. **Sustainability** — promoting environmentally sound and resilient housing development.
4. **Coordination** — aligning housing planning and delivery across agencies and authorities.
5. **Institutional strength** — building capacity and governance for effective housing policy implementation.

## Role in GPPP 2025

DRN 2018–2025 is one of several national policy instruments — alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], and [[entities/dpn2]] — that GPPP 2025 references to ground its state-level housing standards in national policy direction. Its emphasis on accessibility and quality feeds into GPPP 2025's [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]] provisions (OKU, elderly, and childcare considerations across housing typologies) and its coordination thrust supports the alignment of state guidelines with [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] and local authority planning practice under [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].

DRN's accessibility and sustainability thrusts are also echoed in GPPP 2025's affordable housing framework, [[entities/rumah-selangorku]], and in broader [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] applied across housing zones in Selangor.
