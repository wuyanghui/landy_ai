## Overview

The Tabung Amanah Perkuburan Negeri Selangor (Selangor State Cemetery Trust Fund) is a state-administered financial mechanism established to fund the provision and upkeep of burial (perkuburan) facilities in Selangor. It is described in the KM5 (Kemudahan Perkuburan) chapter of the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline as part of the [[concepts/burial-and-cemetery-planning-standards]] framework.

## Function

The fund operates through mandatory developer contributions tied to project scale: housing developments meeting or exceeding 10,000 population or 250 acres are required to contribute to the trust fund. Contribution rates are set per burial plot and vary by district — RM500 per plot in some areas and RM300 per plot in others — providing a standardized cost-sharing mechanism between large-scale developers and the state's obligation to supply adequate burial land.

## Related Mechanisms

The fund's contribution model intersects with racial land quota considerations (Rizab Melayu) embedded in the state's burial land policy, and complements the [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] approach used to size cemetery facilities (from neighbourhood- to district-level) according to projected population and death-rate formulas. It reflects the same population-anchored planning logic applied throughout the guideline's approach to community facility provision.

## Context

As part of Bahagian B of the guideline issued by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]], the trust fund mechanism underscores the state's approach to ensuring burial land keeps pace with rapid housing development, particularly in large master-planned townships.
