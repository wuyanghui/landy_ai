# Rumah Selangorku

**Rumah Selangorku** (Selangorku 3.0) is Selangor's state affordable-housing policy and product line, defined in the *Dasar Perumahan Mampu Milik Negeri Selangor* and given detailed planning standards in [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] (Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025).

## Zoning Framework

The policy divides Selangor into four zones based on urban density and location:

- **Zon 1** — high-density urban areas
- **Zon 2** — urban areas
- **Zon 3** — urban fringe areas
- **Zon 4** — rural areas

Each zone (mapped by district/mukim in Rajah PR(1)) carries different mandatory quota requirements for affordable housing provision, differentiated further by proximity to [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] nodes, TAD, or CBD areas, and by land parcel size (from under 5 acres to over 50 acres). See [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] for the quota mechanics (Jadual PR(2)).

## Housing Components (Jadual PR(3))

Rumah Selangorku 3.0 defines fixed unit types with prescribed specifications:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Solo | Single unit configuration |
| Duo | Two-unit configuration |
| Trio | Three-unit configuration |
| Quad/Teres | Four-unit/terrace configuration |

Each type has a minimum built-up area, prescribed bedroom count, and a fixed selling price ranging from RM114,750 to RM250,000, illustrated with floor plans and building renders in the source document.

## Technical Planning Standards (PR7)

Bahagian B of the guideline sets out **PR7: Rumah Selangorku (Rumah Teres, Rumah Bandar dan Pangsapuri)**, covering:

- Terrace and strata landed housing standards — lot size, floor area, room count, storey/floor-to-floor height, block length, cul-de-sac, back lane/fire break/side lane dimensions, corner-cut, and setbacks.
- Apartment/flat (Pangsapuri) standards — site size, plinth area, density, plot coverage, podium levels, lift and bedroom-count rules.
- Parking standards for cars, motorcycles, quad-bikes, and OKU bays, plus EV charging bay provision.
- Communal and social facilities — surau, funeral management space, non-Muslim worship space, halls, elderly activity spaces, and childcare (Tadika/Taska), reflecting [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]] principles.

## Relation to State Policy

Rumah Selangorku operationalizes housing-affordability objectives set at the national and state level, including [[entities/dpn2]] (which names Rumah Mampu Milik as a strategic target) and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]. It is also cited as one of the six best-practice development initiatives (Perumahan Mampu Milik) in Bahagian A of [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]].
