# New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030)

The New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030) is Malaysia's national policy blueprint guiding the country's industrial development strategy through 2030. It is one of the key national policy instruments referenced in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] as underpinning industrial land use planning in Selangor.

## Structure and Priorities

NIMP 2030 identifies priority and relevant industry sectors and sets sector-specific policies to guide industrial growth. It is organised around four missions:

1. **Economic complexity** — advancing higher-value industrial activity
2. **Technology adoption** — driving digitalisation and modernisation across industry
3. **Net-zero carbon emission pathway** — aligning industrial growth with decarbonisation targets
4. **Economic inclusivity** — ensuring broad-based participation in industrial growth

These missions are operationalised through Mission Based Projects.

## Role in the Selangor Guideline

NIMP 2030 sits alongside other national and state policy instruments — [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/netr]] — as part of the policy foundation cited in Bahagian A of the Selangor industrial planning guideline. Its emphasis on net-zero pathways and technology adoption reinforces the document's [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]] theme, including Eco-Industrial Park practices and climate adaptation measures for industrial development. The guideline was compiled by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
