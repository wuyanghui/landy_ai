# Road Engineering Association of Malaysia (2002)

A technical reference work published in 2002 that establishes the road classification system and design standards used as the primary source for road hierarchy provisions in "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor 2025" (see [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]]).

## Role in the Document

The 2002 publication is cited as the foundational source for the **[[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]]** framework, which divides Malaysian roads into Rural (Luar Bandar, classes R1–R6) and Urban (Bandar, classes U1–U6) categories based on population density (above/below 10,000 people per sq km) and design speed. It underpins:

- The six-tier road classification system (Lebuhraya Ekspres/Expressway, Jalan Arteri/Arterial, Jalan Pengumpul/Collector, Jalan Tempatan/Local, and related urban/rural subtypes)
- The Piawaian Reka Bentuk Jalan Raya (Road Design Standards) table cross-referencing design speed (≥40 km/j to ≥90 km/j), access control levels, traffic flow type, and geometry for each road class
- Minimum road width and reserve standards for national, state, interchange, and junction-level roads across residential, commercial, and industrial land uses

## Status and Updates

The guideline notes that standards derived from this 2002 work remain subject to updates from [[entities/jabatan-kerja-raya-jkr]] and the local authorities (PBT), indicating it functions as a baseline reference rather than a static, unchangeable standard.

## Related

Used alongside other policy instruments compiled by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] to form the technical basis of Selangor's infrastructure planning guidelines.
