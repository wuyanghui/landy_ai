# Fourth National Physical Plan (RFN4)

The Fourth National Physical Plan (Rancangan Fizikal Negara Keempat, RFN4) is one of Malaysia's key national spatial planning instruments. It provides the overarching policy framework for land use and physical development planning across the country, guiding how states and local authorities manage growth in a spatially balanced way.

## Role in Industrial Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]], RFN4 is cited as one of the national policy instruments underpinning Selangor's industrial planning guidelines, alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/nimp2030]], and [[entities/netr]]. Its specific contribution is a focus on **dynamic and balanced industrial growth management** — ensuring that industrial development is not concentrated haphazardly but distributed and managed in line with broader national spatial planning objectives.

## Role in Housing Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]], RFN4 is cited among the national and state policies that the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025* aligns with, alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/drn-2018-2025]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]. Here its contribution centers on **spatial and climate resilience, land use direction, and livable-neighborhood principles (teras)** — informing how housing schemes are sited and structured to support resilient, well-integrated communities. This spatial-resilience emphasis reinforces the housing guideline's broader push toward [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] and structured [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] under frameworks like [[entities/rumah-selangorku]].

## Role in Business/Commercial Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], RFN4 is listed among the six policy and planning frameworks underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor, 2025*, alongside [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]]. Here RFN4 is characterized by three teras (thrusts): **strategic dynamic growth**, **spatial resilience and climate change adaptation**, and a **conducive/livable environment**, the latter explicitly encompassing digital infrastructure and logistics. This positions RFN4 as the source of both the growth-management and resilience logic that recurs across Selangor's sectoral planning guidelines, and — via its digital infrastructure and logistics emphasis — links national physical planning to the smart-city and connectivity ambitions found in the business guideline's best-practice initiatives.

## Role in Infrastructure Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]], RFN4 appears in the Bahagian A policy foundations alongside [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] as one of the national instruments underpinning Selangor's infrastructure standards. In this document RFN4 is framed around three thrusts — **dynamic and balanced growth**, **digital and smart infrastructure**, and **carbon-neutral development** — each with supporting policy directions and key indicators. This framing echoes the growth-and-resilience logic seen in the business and housing guidelines but foregrounds infrastructure-specific priorities: the digital/smart thrust supports telecommunications tower siting and smart-city ambitions, while the carbon-neutral thrust reinforces the guideline's push toward clean-energy and electric-vehicle infrastructure.

## Role in Community Facilities Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], RFN4 is cited as one of the national and state policy instruments — alongside [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dpn2]] — underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025*. Here RFN4 is represented through **Teras 3 (Persekitaran Berdaya Huni dan Komuniti / Livable Environment and Community)**, comprising two Key Initiatives: **KI 2**, building an inclusive, competitive, education-based society, and **KI 3**, fostering a conducive and livable environment, each with defined sub-targets. This teras grounds the guideline's emphasis on equitable, accessible facility distribution and reinforces its [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] approach to siting health, education, safety, religious, and recreational facilities, while its livable-environment focus resonates with the guideline's [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] safety principles and its push toward [[concepts/vertical-facility-development]] in land-constrained urban areas.

## Role in Environmental Planning

Within [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]], RFN4 is introduced as Malaysia's key national physical planning strategic document/master plan guiding sustainable physical development, and is presented through **Teras 2: Kemampanan Spatial dan Daya Perubahan Tahan Iklim (Spatial Sustainability and Climate Resilience)**. This thrust is broken into key result areas (KD): **KD1 Perancangan Guna Tanah Holistik**, covering balanced development, flood/disaster resilience, and climate change impact; **KD2 Pengurusan Mampan Sumber Asli, Sumber Makanan**, covering coastal and marine ecosystem management, control of Environmentally Sensitive Areas ([[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]]), river corridor rehabilitation (connecting to the guideline's [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] standards), and Sponge City concepts; and a partially documented **KD3**, addressing wildfire/peatland fire risk and land subsidence. Here RFN4's climate-resilience and holistic land-use logic directly underpins the guideline's disaster risk reduction principles, its natural resource conservation standards for hills, lakes, and coastlines, and — via the Sponge City concept — its promotion of water-sensitive design measures such as [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]].

## Relationship to State-Level Planning

RFN4 sits above state-level instruments such as [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] in Malaysia's planning hierarchy, providing the national-level direction that state structure plans and, in turn, sectoral guidelines like Selangor's industrial, housing, business/commercial, infrastructure, community facilities, and environmental planning standards (coordinated by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]]), are expected to align with.
