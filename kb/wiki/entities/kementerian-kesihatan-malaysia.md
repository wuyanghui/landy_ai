# Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia (Ministry of Health Malaysia)

Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia (KKM), the Ministry of Health Malaysia, is the federal government body responsible for public healthcare policy, hospital and clinic operations, and health facility planning standards nationwide.

## Role in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]]

KKM is the primary reference agency underpinning **KM1: Kemudahan Kesihatan** (Health Facilities) in the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025. The guideline's health facility standards are built around KKM's own classification and service hierarchy:

- **Hospital tiers**: Institusi Perubatan Khas/Institusi Khas, Hospital Negeri, Hospital Pakar Major, Hospital Pakar Minor, and Hospital Tanpa Pakar — each defined by sub-specialist vs. specialist vs. basic care scope, tied to [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] (from Bandar Global/Wilayah at >1.5 million population down to Bandar Tempatan at 20,000–100,000).
- **Klinik Kesihatan (health clinic) tiers**: Jenis 1–7, classified by catchment population within a 5km radius, daily patient visits, and gross floor area, ranging from major urban clinics to small Klinik Komuniti serving under 5,000 residents.

The guideline explicitly notes that development proposals for hospitals and clinics require early consultation with KKM regarding site suitability, service requirements, and supporting documentation, reflecting KKM's regulatory authority over health facility siting and design standards even when land-use planning is led by local authorities (PBT).

## Related Standards

Health facility site planning under KKM's framework intersects with several cross-cutting planning requirements in the guideline, including [[concepts/parking-standards]] (staff, visitor, ambulance, and patient vehicle ratios), [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]], and [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] (internal and access road widths for hospitals and clinics).
