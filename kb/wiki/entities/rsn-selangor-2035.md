# RSN Selangor 2035

Rancangan Struktur Negeri Selangor 2035 (Selangor Structural Plan 2035) is the state-level statutory land use plan that shapes long-term spatial development in Selangor, including the placement and management of industrial land, the direction of housing policy, the framework for commercial/business development, the planning basis for state infrastructure provision, the strategic direction for community facility planning, and the structural basis for environmental planning and conservation.

## Role in Industrial Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] as one of the key policy instruments underpinning the state's industrial planning guidelines. It addresses:

- **Water catchment protection** — restricting or conditioning industrial development near water catchment areas
- **Public transport integration** — aligning industrial land planning with public transport accessibility
- **Industrial land use planning** — providing the structural framework within which industrial zones, including those governed by the type-specific standards in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]], are designated and managed

## Role in Housing Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is also cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] as one of the state-level policies that the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025* aligns with. It structures its housing direction around 3 Teras Wilayah Bandar Makmur (Prosperous Urban Region Thrusts) and 5 Hala Tuju Strategik (MM1–MM5), covering housing type and cost, home ownership, rental housing, green technology, and safety. This state-level structural guidance feeds into the [[entities/bandar-berdaya-huni-negeri-selangor]] livable-city framework and supports affordable housing provisioning under [[entities/rumah-selangorku]] and [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]].

## Role in Business and Commercial Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is also cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (as a draft state structural plan) among the six policy frameworks underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor, 2025*, alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]]. For business and commercial development, RSN Selangor 2035 is organized around three teras (thrusts):

- **Dynamic and competitive state** — driving business growth and economic competitiveness
- **Sustainable and resilient state** — green infrastructure and climate-responsive development
- **Resource-efficient and livable state** — balancing housing, social wellbeing, and economic activity

This structural guidance frames the general commercial siting and design principles set out in Bahagian A of [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], which in turn govern the type-specific technical standards (parking, setbacks, accessibility) applied across the twenty commercial development categories in that document.

## Role in Infrastructure Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is also cited in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] as one of the state-level policy instruments underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor, 2025*, alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and the [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]]. For infrastructure planning, RSN Selangor 2035 is organized around three teras (thrusts) — a Well-Developed Region, an Advanced Region, and a Prosperous Region — each with associated measures for telecommunications, utilities, transportation, technology, and services infrastructure. This structural guidance underpins the technical standards set out across the document's specific guideline chapters, including road hierarchy and design under [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] (built on classifications from the [[entities/road-engineering-association-of-malaysia]]), electrical substation and transmission corridor siting under [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]] (coordinated with [[entities/tenaga-nasional-berhad-tnb]]), shared utility corridor planning under [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]], and electric vehicle charging bay provision under [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]].

## Role in Community Facilities Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is also cited in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] as one of the state-level policy instruments underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025*, alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], and [[entities/dpn2]]. For community facilities planning, RSN Selangor 2035 is organized around three development thrusts — Efficient Resource Hub, Livable, and Competitive — with two strategic directions of particular relevance: Hala Tuju Strategik 2 (MM7–MM10), covering education, health, and safety facilities, and Hala Tuju Strategik 3 (MM11–MM14), covering community, religious, recreation, and sports facilities. This structural guidance underpins the population-based facility hierarchies described in [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] and the safety-by-design principles under [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] that recur across the document's KM1–KM9 facility-specific standards, including health, education, safety, religious, burial, community, library, open space, market, and transport terminal facilities.

## Role in Environmental Planning

RSN Selangor 2035 is also cited in [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] as one of the state-level policy instruments underpinning the *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor, 2025*, alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[entities/dasn]]. For environmental planning, RSN Selangor 2035 sets out a strategic direction for sustainable urban regional development encompassing Environmentally Sensitive Areas ([[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]]), coastal zone management, integrated and efficient river basin management, and green technology adoption in the energy and water supply sectors. This structural guidance underpins the technical standards set out across the document's special guideline chapters (Bahagian B), including hillside and highland development controls, natural and man-made lake preservation zoning, tree preservation orders, coastal development setbacks, river reserve development standards under [[concepts/river-reserve-development]], and mandatory rainwater harvesting system (SPAH) requirements under [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]].

## Relation to Other Policy Instruments

RSN Selangor 2035 operates alongside national-level frameworks such as [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/nimp2030]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/dasn]], [[entities/netr]], and the [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]] to form the combined policy foundation described in [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]]. As the state structural plan, it provides the spatial planning basis that local authorities under [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] use to implement industrial siting decisions, including [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]] between industrial and other land uses; housing planning standards, including transit-oriented density principles described in [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] and coordinated with [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]]; commercial development standards such as [[concepts/parking-standards]], [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]], and [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] applied across business and industrial land uses alike; state infrastructure standards spanning road design, electrical and telecommunications siting, and utility corridor provision; community facility standards spanning the population-based hierarchies and accessibility requirements applied across health, education, safety, religious, burial, and recreational facility categories; and environmental planning standards spanning hillside/highland development control, Environmentally Sensitive Area designation ([[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]]), lake and coastal preservation, river reserve development ([[concepts/river-reserve-development]]), and rainwater harvesting system requirements ([[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]]).
