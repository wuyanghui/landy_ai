# Dasar Perancangan Pembangunan Berorientasikan Transit (TOD) Negeri Selangor

## Overview

The Dasar Perancangan Pembangunan Berorientasikan Transit (TOD) Negeri Selangor is the state of Selangor's transit-oriented development policy, first established in 2016 and gazetted in the same year. It sets out principles for planning development — including commercial and business uses — around transit stations and catchment zones, promoting density, walkability, and multimodal connectivity.

## Role in PEQ-Perniagaan

Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], the Dasar TOD is cited in the Bahagian A policy framework (Section 3.0, Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) as one of six national and state policy instruments underpinning the guideline's approach to business and commercial development planning, alongside the [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]].

## Core Principles

The policy is built on nine core principles applicable within transit catchment zones:

- Mixed-use development combining residential, commercial, and institutional uses
- Higher development density near transit stations
- Provision of affordable housing and affordable commercial units within TOD zones
- Pedestrian and cycling path networks connecting development to transit nodes
- Rationalized parking provision reflecting reduced car dependency
- Feeder bus services linking developments to rail/transit stations
- Promotion of green building practices within TOD precincts

## Relevance to Business Planning

By prioritizing accessibility to transit (LRT/MRT/bus terminals) as a siting criterion, the Dasar TOD directly informs the general siting standards in [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (Section 6.0, Piawaian Umum), which requires strategic, accessible locations for commercial developments near CBDs and transit infrastructure. It also aligns with the broader [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] approach applied across Selangor's planning documents, including [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]].
