## Overview

Jabatan Bomba dan Penyelamat Malaysia (JBPM), the Fire and Rescue Department, is the national statutory body responsible for fire safety, firefighting infrastructure, and rescue services in Malaysia. Within [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], JBPM (referred to throughout as "Bomba") is cited as the approving and standard-setting authority for fire safety provisions across all twenty categories of commercial and business development covered by the guideline. Within [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], JBPM is additionally the operating agency and planning authority for fire and rescue stations (Balai Bomba dan Penyelamat) as a category of community safety facility (KM3-3).

## Role in Commercial Development (PEQ-Perniagaan)

Across nearly every development type in Bahagian B — including terrace shops (PN1), affordable terrace shops (PN2), business complexes (PN4), MICE centres (PN5), hotels (PN6), hypermarkets (PN7), service stations (PN8), bazaars (PN9), rest stops (PN10), SOHO (PN12), service apartments (PN13), private hospitals (PN14), private schools (PN15), IPTS (PN16), theme parks (PN17), golf courses (PN18), clubhouses (PN19), and sports complexes (PN20) — the guideline mandates:

- Provision of fire hydrants and firefighting apparatus in accordance with Bomba requirements
- Compliance with [[entities/ms1184]] for accessible fire safety and evacuation design for OKU (disabled) users
- Building height controls partly determined by JBPM firefighting access requirements (e.g., minimum service road widths for fire apparatus, siting relative to fire lanes)
- For private hospitals specifically, fire and firefighting apparatus space is required to occupy a minimum of 5% of site area
- Affordable terrace shop developments require fire extinguishing equipment placement approved by the State Fire and Rescue Department

## Role in Community Facility Planning (PEQ-K-Masyarakat)

Under KM3-3 (Balai Bomba dan Penyelamat) of the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025, JBPM is the agency for which fire and rescue stations are planned as part of the [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] of community safety infrastructure, alongside police facilities. Key standards include:

- **Five-tier station classification (Category A–E)**, based on service area type and population served — from over 100,000 people (Category A, global/main city) down to community/village-level stations (Category E) — each with corresponding minimum site area, built-up floor area, building height, and setback/reserve requirements
- **Siting governed by the JBPM Fire Risk Analysis Grid Map**, requiring alignment with local plans, a minimum 75km spacing between stations, avoidance of environmentally sensitive areas and geologically unstable/ex-mining/swamp land, and [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] (BPGT) approval from [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]] for Class III/IV land
- **Response time standards** of 5–20 minutes depending on area category, tied to minimum access [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] widths for internal roads, service roads, and adjoining roads
- **Facility components** including the station building, dormitory/staff quarters, admin building, multipurpose hall, logistics store, drill yard, watch tower, and covered parking, plus supporting elements such as a recreational field, guard post, fire equipment store, and signage
- **[[concepts/parking-standards]]** for fire trucks, staff quarters, and motorcycles, plus [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] and [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] provisions
- Illustrated by JBPM Selangor State Headquarters as the real-world reference example (Rajah KM3-3(1))

## Related Standards

JBPM requirements are consistently paired with [[entities/ms1184]] accessibility compliance and are one of the recurring technical thresholds — alongside [[concepts/parking-standards]], [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]], [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]], and [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] — that both commercial developments and community safety facilities in Selangor must satisfy before approval.
