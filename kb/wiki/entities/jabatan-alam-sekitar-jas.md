# Jabatan Alam Sekitar (JAS)

Jabatan Alam Sekitar (Department of Environment, JAS) is the federal agency responsible for environmental regulation and pollution control in Malaysia. It appears throughout [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] as one of the technical agencies whose guidelines and approvals underpin Selangor's environmental planning standards.

## Role in the Document

- **Legal/technical reference source**: JAS guidelines are cited among the 29 legal and technical references (Punca Kuasa / Peruntukan Perundangan) that form the legislative basis for the guideline, alongside other technical departments such as [[entities/jabatan-kerja-raya-jkr]], [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]], and [[entities/jabatan-pengairan-dan-saliran-jps]].
- **Coastal industry approvals**: under coastal development guidelines (AS4), special industry developments near the coast (e.g. petrochemical facilities) require approval from JAS alongside JPS, JMG, the Fisheries Department, and MPFN, reflecting its role in controlling pollution risk from industrial siting.
- **River reserve coordination**: under [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] (AS5), JAS is named among the relevant authorities (with JPS, LUAS, PBT, PBN) whose coordination is required for pollution control and development approvals affecting river corridors.

## Significance

JAS's recurring presence across the hillside/highland, coastal, and river reserve sections underscores its cross-cutting mandate: pollution prevention and environmental compliance are treated as a standing referral requirement for development proposals touching environmentally sensitive areas ([[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]]), rather than being confined to a single guideline section.
