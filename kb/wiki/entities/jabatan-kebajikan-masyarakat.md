# Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat (Department of Social Welfare)

Jabatan Kebajikan Masyarakat (JKM) is the Malaysian federal department responsible for social welfare services, referenced in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] as the governing/coordinating body for community-based welfare facilities under KM6: Kemudahan Komuniti.

## Role in Community Facility Planning

JKM oversees establishment and operational standards for several facility types defined in the guideline's KM6-2 provisions:

- **Pusat Aktiviti Warga Emas (PAWE)** — elderly activity centres, established under committees operating with reference to the Selangor State Government Secretary's Office, with JKM involvement in governance and standards.
- **Pusat Pemulihan Dalam Komuniti (PPDK)** — community-based rehabilitation centres for persons with disabilities (OKU), governed by Pejabat Kebajikan Masyarakat Daerah (District Social Welfare Office), a JKM field arm.
- **Pusat Aktiviti Kanak-Kanak (PAKK)** — children's activity centres, involving JKM-linked referral and registration processes for OKU participants.

The guideline specifies minimum site area (0.2–1.0 acre depending on facility type), a population catchment of 10,000 residents, and building components (offices, activity rooms, multi-sensory rooms, counselling rooms) for these JKM-linked facilities, alongside standard [[concepts/parking-standards]] and [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] requirements.
