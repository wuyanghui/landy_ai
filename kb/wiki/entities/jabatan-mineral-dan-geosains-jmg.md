# Jabatan Mineral dan Geosains (JMG)

Jabatan Mineral dan Geosains (JMG) — the Department of Minerals and Geoscience — is a federal Malaysian government agency responsible for assessing land stability and geological suitability for development. Across multiple PLANMalaysia Selangor guidelines, including [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] and [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]], JMG is the issuing authority for the **Borang Pengesahan Geologi Tapak (BPGT)**, a mandatory geological site clearance certificate required before siting development on land classified as Kawasan Sensitif Alam Sekitar (KSAS) Class III/IV, former mining land, peat, or other soft/unstable soil.

## Role in Community Facility Planning

In the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline (Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025), BPGT clearance from JMG is a recurring precondition across nearly every community facility category (KM1–KM9), including:

- **Schools (KM2)** — primary schools, secondary schools, and integrated school complexes require geological clearance as part of site selection, particularly where BPGT/geological safety assessment is flagged.
- **Kindergartens and nurseries (KM2-4)** — landed tadika/taska sites outside KSAS require a BPGT from JMG before approval.
- **Religious facilities (KM4)** — mosques, surau, and non-Islamic places of worship must avoid former mining, quarry, or unstable land, or obtain BPGT clearance for KSAS Class III/IV and peat/soft soil zones.
- **Safety facilities (KM3)** — police contingent and district headquarters (IPK/IPD), police stations/posts, and fire and rescue stations sited on Class III/IV land or peat require JMG's geological suitability verification.
- **Burial facilities (KM5)** — cemetery, crematorium, and columbarium siting includes geotechnical (BPGT) approval requirements tied to soil structure, topography, and groundwater protection.

## Role in Environmental Planning (Hillside, Highland, and Peat Areas)

In [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] (Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor, 2025), JMG's remit extends beyond issuing BPGT clearance to authoring the underlying geological reports that inform it:

- **AS1-1 (Hillside and Highland Development, KSAS)** — JMG's technical guidelines on geology and slope form part of the reference framework governing development control by terrain class (Kelas I–IV) and elevation band, and hillside proposals in higher-risk classes may require referral to the JKTPKSAS technical committee.
- **AS1-2 (Peat and Soft Soil Areas)** — Development proposals on peat land must appoint a **registered geologist consultant from Jabatan Mineral dan Geosains Malaysia** to prepare a comprehensive **Surface and Subsurface Geology Report**, covering topography and lithology, land use zoning, geotechnical characteristics, engineering properties, and hydrology. This report works alongside a separately required Geotechnical Report (prepared by a registered engineer) covering soil strength parameters and drainage/disposal methods for peat areas.

This positions JMG as both a certifying authority (BPGT) and a source of specialist geological reporting expertise feeding into disaster risk reduction assessments across Selangor's hillside, highland, and peat soil development controls.

## Related Concepts

JMG's clearance function underpins the [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] requirement that recurs throughout Selangor's community facility, industrial, commercial, housing, infrastructure, and environmental planning guidelines as a cross-cutting site-suitability control, closely tied to [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] classification, alongside other siting constraints such as [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]] and [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]].
