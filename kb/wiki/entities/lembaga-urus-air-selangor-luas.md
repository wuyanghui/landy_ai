# Lembaga Urus Air Selangor (LUAS)

Lembaga Urus Air Selangor (LUAS) is the Selangor state statutory body responsible for water resource management, including regulation of rivers, water catchments, and river reserves. Within [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]], LUAS is cited as one of the technical authorities whose standards and approvals underpin the state's environmental planning guidelines.

## Role in the Guideline

LUAS is referenced primarily in connection with [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] (AS5), where river reserve width determination and development conditions along rivers are established with reference to LUAS standards alongside those of Jabatan Pengairan dan Saliran ([[entities/jabatan-pengairan-dan-saliran-jps]]) and PLANMalaysia Selangor ([[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]]). Development proposals affecting river corridors, waterfront structures, and river control zones require coordination with LUAS for approval, reflecting its jurisdiction over water body management in the state.

## Related Coordination

LUAS operates alongside other technical agencies named in the guideline's legal and institutional framework, including [[entities/jabatan-pengairan-dan-saliran-jps]] and [[entities/jabatan-alam-sekitar-jas]], particularly on matters of river reserve widths, riverbank protection, and water quality safeguards described under [[concepts/river-reserve-development]].
