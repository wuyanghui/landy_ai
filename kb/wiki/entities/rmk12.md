# 12th Malaysia Plan (RMK12)

The 12th Malaysia Plan (RMK12, 2021–2025) is Malaysia's national five-year development blueprint, cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]], [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]], [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]], [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]], [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], and [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] as one of the core policy instruments underpinning Selangor's industrial, housing, business/commercial, infrastructure, community facilities, and environmental planning guidelines.

## Key Themes Relevant to Industrial Planning

- **Theme 1: Economic Revitalisation** — RMK12 prioritises reviving and strengthening the national economy, with industrial development framed as a key lever.
- **Sector Focus** — Emphasis on high-value services, manufacturing, and agriculture sectors as growth drivers.
- **Eco-Industrial Parks** — RMK12 promotes eco-industrial park models and green/clean industry practices, directly informing [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]] principles adopted in the Selangor guideline.
- **MSME and R&D Support** — The plan supports micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and research & development initiatives within the industrial sector.

## Key Themes Relevant to Housing Planning

- **Housing Sector Focus Areas** — RMK12 sets out five focus areas for housing: supply, financing access, governance, inclusive housing, and sustainable urban development.
- **Alignment with GPPP 2025** — The Selangor State Housing Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025 (GPPP 2025) cite RMK12 alongside [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/drn-2018-2025]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] as part of the national and state policy stack shaping affordable housing provision, exemplified by initiatives such as [[entities/rumah-selangorku]] and [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]].

## Key Themes Relevant to Business/Commercial Planning

- **Smart City Development** — RMK12 prioritises smart technology, open data, and IoT adoption, cited in the Selangor business guideline's policy foundation (Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) as a driver of digital and e-commerce-ready commercial infrastructure.
- **Infrastructure Upgrades** — The plan calls for improvements to roads, utilities, and digitalisation to support commercial and business activity.
- **SME Support** — RMK12 backs SME development through improved financing access, business support, and skills training, echoed in the business guideline's SME empowerment initiatives.
- **Services Sector Development** — The plan emphasises growth of the services sector, aligning with the guideline's focus on hierarchical, diversified commercial development.

## Key Themes Relevant to Infrastructure Planning

The Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor 2025 (GPPP Infrastruktur) cites RMK12 in its Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan (Section 3.0) as one of six governing policy instruments, drawing on six core thrusts:

- **Connectivity** — improved road, transport, and network linkages, feeding into [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] standards.
- **Quality of Life** — infrastructure provision that raises everyday living standards.
- **Service Delivery Infrastructure** — efficient provision of utilities and public facilities, echoed in [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] and [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]].
- **Competitiveness** — infrastructure as an enabler of economic performance.
- **Digital and Green Infrastructure** — supporting smart city initiatives and clean energy adoption, reflected in the guideline's [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] provisions.
- **Resilience** — infrastructure designed to withstand and adapt to environmental and operational pressures.

In this guideline, RMK12 is grouped with the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], the [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] as the policy foundation (Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) justifying technical standards spanning road design, parking, electrical substations, telecommunications towers, utility corridors, and sewerage treatment plants.

## Key Themes Relevant to Community Facilities Planning

The Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025 (GPPP Kemudahan Masyarakat) cites RMK12 in Section 3.2 of its Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan, identifying four key linkages to community facility provision:

- **Access to Health and Education** — RMK12 prioritises equitable access to health and education facilities, directly informing the health ([[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]]-tiered clinics and hospitals) and education (schools, IPTA) standards in the guideline.
- **Inclusivity and Regional Equity** — the plan's emphasis on balanced regional development underpins the guideline's push for equitable facility distribution across high-density and less-populated areas.
- **Emergency and Public Service Facilities** — RMK12 supports investment in emergency and public service infrastructure, aligning with the guideline's safety facility standards (police stations, fire and rescue stations).
- **Social Community Facility Development** — the plan's social development priorities are reflected in the guideline's community centre, library, and recreational facility provisions.

Here, RMK12 sits alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rfn4]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] as part of the national/state policy stack (Section 3.0) that justifies the guideline's population-based, inclusive standards for health, education, safety, religious, burial, community, library, recreation, market, and transport terminal facilities.

## Key Themes Relevant to Environmental Planning

The Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor 2025 devotes its own subsection (3.2, within Section 3.0 Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) to RMK12, framing it as emphasising environmental conservation and inclusive, sustainable development nationwide through four core environmental strategies:

- **Conserving Natural Ecosystems (Memulihara Ekosistem Semula Jadi)** — strengthening restoration measures for terrestrial and coastal areas, reinforcing [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] controls.
- **Sustainable Use and Benefit-Sharing (Memastikan Penggunaan Mampan dan Perkongsian Faedah)** — strengthening sustainable use of natural resources and diversifying conservation funding.
- **Improving Water Resource Conservation (Meningkatkan Pemuliharaan Sumber Air)** — strengthening water resource management and addressing water pollution, echoed in the guideline's [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] and [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]] standards.
- **Strengthening Environmental Governance (Memperkukuh Tadbir Urus Alam Sekitar)** — improving policies, legislation, and local authority enforcement.

In this guideline, RMK12 is presented alongside the [[entities/sustainable-development-goals]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and the Selangor State Structure Plan 2035 as part of the Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan (Section 3.0) that grounds the technical standards for hillside/highland development, lake preservation, tree preservation, coastal development, river reserves, and rainwater harvesting set out in Bahagian B.

## Role in the Guidelines

RMK12 is listed among the policy foundations that [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] draws upon to justify and align the state's industrial, housing, business, infrastructure, community facilities, and environmental planning standards with national development priorities. In the industrial guideline, it is grouped with [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/nimp2030]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/netr]], particularly around green industry practices and balanced economic growth. In the housing guideline, it anchors housing supply, financing, and inclusive urban development priorities that feed into the [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] and affordable housing frameworks adopted state-wide. In the business guideline, it is grouped with the sustainable development goals, [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], and [[entities/dasar-tod-selangor]] as part of the six-framework policy basis (Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) underpinning smart, digitally-enabled, and SME-friendly commercial development across Selangor. In the infrastructure guideline, it anchors the connectivity, resilience, and digital/green infrastructure priorities that shape road, parking, electrical, telecommunications, utility corridor, and sewerage standards, working alongside references such as [[entities/road-engineering-association-of-malaysia]], [[entities/jabatan-kerja-raya-jkr]], and [[entities/tenaga-nasional-berhad-tnb]]. In the community facilities guideline, it anchors equitable access to health, education, safety, and social facilities, reinforcing the population-based hierarchy and inclusive distribution principles that govern facilities from [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy|clinics and schools]] to community centres and libraries. In the environmental guideline, it anchors ecosystem conservation, sustainable resource use, water resource management, and environmental governance priorities that underpin the technical standards for hillsides, lakes, trees, coastlines, river reserves, and rainwater harvesting.
