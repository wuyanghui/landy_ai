# Strata Titles Act 1985 (Act 318)

The Strata Titles Act 1985 (Act 318) is the Malaysian federal legislation that governs the subdivision of buildings and land into strata parcels, defines common property, and regulates the management of strata schemes. It is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] as the legal basis for several housing typologies, and in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] as the framework governing community facility provision within strata-titled residential developments.

## Role in the Selangor Housing Guidelines

Act 318 underpins the planning standards for strata-titled housing types set out in Bahagian B of the housing guidelines, distinguishing them from conventional landed housing:

- **Perumahan Strata (PR4 — landed strata housing)**: applies to landed housing not exceeding 4 storeys where units are subdivided under strata title, requiring compliance with strata subdivision rules alongside physical planning standards.
- **Rumah Kluster Berkembar (PR5)**: a cluster/semi-detached scheme structured as a strata scheme under the Act, with shared common property and communal facilities.
- **Pangsapuri Harga Bebas/Kondominium (PR6)**: free-market apartments and condominiums, inherently strata-titled multi-storey buildings with shared common property (surau, recreational areas, security).
- **Rumah Selangorku Pangsapuri (PR7)**: affordable apartment schemes under [[entities/rumah-selangorku]] also fall under strata title arrangements.

## Role in the Community Facilities Guidelines

[[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] extends strata-title considerations to community facility provision within stratified developments:

- **Tadika dan Taska (KM2-4)**: for strata developments, minimum floor area for kindergartens/nurseries is calculated per housing unit count (e.g., 55.74 m² for 200 units, 74.32 m² for 250–400 units, plus 0.186 m² per additional unit above 400), with facilities sited at ground floor/podium level and served by dedicated ramps, loading bays, and pedestrian access separated from vehicle/logistics routes.
- **Rumah Ibadat Selain Islam (KM4-3)**: stratified residential developments must provide a neutral prayer/meditation room (approx. 46.45 m²) at a ratio of 1 room per 5,000 population/1,250 units, reflecting the Act's common-property framework for shared religious facilities.
- **Kompleks Komuniti/Pusat Komuniti (KM6-1)**: vertical/high-rise community complex developments integrate multiple facilities (nurseries, clinics, libraries, welfare services) by floor within a single strata-managed building, following the same common-property and management-corporation logic as strata housing.

## Key Concepts Defined

The Act's core concepts — parcels, common property, and management corporations — are referenced in both guidelines' glossaries (e.g., "Skim Strata") and inform requirements for shared facilities, guard posts, open space, and management office provisions across strata schemes, whether residential or community-facility-oriented.

## Relevance

The distinction between strata and non-strata (landed) development under this Act shapes differentiated open space, facility-sizing, and management standards throughout both [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] and [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], both issued by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
