# Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are the United Nations' global framework of goals for social, economic, and environmental development, adopted as one of the foundational policy frameworks underpinning multiple Selangor planning guidelines, including [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (commercial and business development), [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] (infrastructure planning), [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] (community facilities planning), and [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] (environmental planning).

## Role in PEQ-Perniagaan

In Section 3.0 (Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan) of the PEQ-Perniagaan document, seven SDGs are identified as directly relevant to business and commercial planning:

- Affordable and Clean Energy
- Decent Work and Economic Growth
- Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- Sustainable Cities and Communities
- Responsible Consumption and Production
- Climate Action
- Partnerships for the Goals

These goals are cited alongside national and state policy instruments — [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] — as the layered policy basis guiding the guideline's approach to business development standards.

## Role in PEQ-infrastruktur

In the Dasar dan Polisi Pembangunan section of PEQ-infrastruktur (Bahagian A), five SDGs are identified as the policy foundation for infrastructure planning:

- Clean Water and Sanitation (SDG 6)
- Affordable and Clean Energy (SDG 7)
- Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure (SDG 9)
- Sustainable Cities and Communities (SDG 11)
- Climate Action (SDG 13)

Here too, the SDGs are positioned as the first layer in a stack of policy instruments alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], the [[entities/pkjr-road-safety-plan]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], collectively shaping standards for road hierarchy, parking, electrical substations, telecommunications towers, utility corridors, and sewerage infrastructure.

## Role in PEQ-K-Masyarakat

Section 3.1 (Matlamat Pembangunan Mampan) of the community facilities guideline identifies seven SDGs as directly relevant to the planning of community facilities:

- No Poverty
- Good Health and Well-Being
- Quality Education
- Decent Work and Economic Growth
- Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- Reduced Inequalities
- Sustainable Cities and Communities

These are presented as the first of six layered policy foundations — alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]], [[entities/dpn2]], and [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] — justifying the guideline's standards for health, education, safety, religious, burial, community, library, open space, market, and transport terminal facilities (KM1–KM9). Here the SDG emphasis shifts toward poverty reduction, health and education access, and inequality reduction, reflecting the guideline's focus on equitable [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] distribution and [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] inclusivity across facility types.

## Role in PEQ-alam-sekitar

Section 3.1 (Matlamat Pembangunan Mampan) of the environmental planning guideline identifies ten SDGs as relevant to environmental planning and development, the broadest set of any of the four guidelines:

- Good Health and Well-Being (reducing pollution-related health risks)
- Quality Education
- Clean Water and Sanitation (protecting water-related ecosystems)
- Affordable and Clean Energy
- Sustainable Cities and Communities (including protection of cultural/natural heritage and access to green and public spaces)
- Responsible Consumption and Production
- Climate Action (resilience and adaptive capacity to climate-related hazards)
- Life Below Water (conserving marine and coastal ecosystems)
- Life on Land (protecting terrestrial ecosystems, forests, and land)
- Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (accountable institutions)

These goals are presented as the first of the document's layered national/state policy stack, alongside [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/rfn4]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/dasn]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]. The emphasis on Life Below Water and Life on Land is distinctive to this guideline, reflecting its focus on hillside/highland development, lake preservation, tree preservation, coastal zones, river reserves, and water efficiency — a broader ecological scope than the goal subsets cited in the other three guidelines.

## Thematic Connections

The SDG priorities are reflected throughout all four guidelines' technical standards. In PEQ-Perniagaan, they inform provisions for [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]], [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]], and site standards promoting [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]]-aligned, resource-efficient commercial development. In PEQ-infrastruktur, the same priorities underpin [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]] and [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] standards, EV charging bay requirements, and the broader [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] framework. In PEQ-K-Masyarakat, the SDGs' emphasis on health, education, and reduced inequalities underpins [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]]-verified siting, [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]], [[concepts/parking-standards]], and [[concepts/vertical-facility-development]] approaches for land-constrained facilities. In PEQ-alam-sekitar, the SDGs' emphasis on clean water, climate resilience, and life on land/below water underpins [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] classification, [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] standards, and [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]] requirements — together reflecting a consistent emphasis on clean energy, industry innovation, sustainable urban communities, inclusivity, ecological conservation, and climate resilience across Selangor's planning documents.
