# National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR)

The National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR) is a Malaysian national policy framework outlining the country's pathway toward a sustainable and low-carbon energy future. It is cited in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] as one of the key policy instruments underpinning Selangor's industrial planning guidelines, particularly regarding energy efficiency and renewable energy adoption in industrial areas.

## Renewable Energy Targets

NETR sets progressive national renewable energy (RE) capacity targets:

- **31%** by 2025
- **40%** by 2035
- **70%** by 2050

## Six Strategic Energy Levers

The roadmap identifies six strategic levers to drive the energy transition:

1. Energy Efficiency
2. Renewable Energy
3. Hydrogen
4. Bioenergy
5. Green Mobility
6. Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage (CCUS)

## Relevance to Industrial Planning

Within the Selangor industrial planning guideline, NETR's targets inform provisions for renewable and efficient energy use in industrial areas, such as solar panel installations and other green technology adoption, aligning with the guideline's broader emphasis on [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]] and Eco-Industrial Park (EIP) practices. It is referenced alongside other national and state policy instruments including [[entities/rmk12]], [[entities/dpn2]], [[entities/rfn4]], and [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] as part of the policy foundation for the guidelines published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
