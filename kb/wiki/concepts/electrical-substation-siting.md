# Electrical Substation Siting Standards

## Overview

Electrical substation siting standards govern where and how electricity infrastructure — from main intake substations to distribution substations and TNB transmission corridors — can be located within development areas. In Selangor, these standards are set out in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] under Section PE (Pencawang Elektrik), covering both substation buildings (PE1) and overhead transmission line reserves (PE2, known as *rentis TNB*).

## Substation Types and Site Requirements (PE1)

The guidelines classify electrical substations into two broad categories:

- **Pencawang Masuk Utama (PMU)** — Main Intake Substations
- **Pencawang Pembahagian Utama (PPU)** — Main Distribution Substations

Additional facility types include Stesen Suis Utama (SSU) and general Pencawang Elektrik (PE), spanning voltage classes from 11kV up to 132/33/11kV, and switchgear technologies including Air Insulated Switchgear (AIS) and Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS), in indoor, outdoor, conventional, single/double chamber, and compact configurations.

For each substation type, the standards specify:

- **Site area and building footprint** requirements, which scale with voltage class and switchgear technology
- **Siting conditions**, including:
  - Minimum 100m setback distance from sewerage treatment plants
  - Flood-prone area platform level requirements (9.14m / 30ft)
  - Access road widths of 6.10m (20ft), with accelerating/decelerating lanes required for PMU sites
- Compliance with [[entities/tenaga-nasional-berhad-tnb]]'s own Piawaian Rekabentuk Pencawang Elektrik (Substation Design Standards)

Final authority over site and building size adjustments, and over final siting decisions generally, rests with [[entities/tenaga-nasional-berhad-tnb]] rather than the local authority alone.

## Transmission Corridor Reserves (PE2 — Rentis TNB)

Separately from substation buildings, the guidelines set reservation (*rizab rentis*) requirements for overhead transmission towers and lines:

- **Minimum building setback of 30.48m (100ft)** from each leg of a transmission tower (pylon) — no buildings or pipelines are permitted within this zone
- **Corridor width by voltage class**:
  - 33kV / 132kV / 275kV lines: 20m reserve on each side of the centerline (30m including forest reserve buffer)
  - 500kV lines: 25m reserve on each side of the centerline (35m including forest reserve buffer)
- Rules governing road, drainage, and pipeline construction within the corridor, including slope-cutting limits and crossing-angle requirements for utility lines that must cross beneath or near transmission corridors

## Relationship to Other Standards

Substation siting interacts directly with other infrastructure planning concerns in the guidelines:

- The 100m setback from sewerage treatment plants links substation planning to sewage facility buffer requirements — a shared theme with [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]]
- Underground electrical cabling within road reserves is coordinated through [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]], which sets spacing requirements between electrical cable and other utility lines (water, sewerage, gas, communications)
- Access road provisions for substations tie into the broader [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] framework governing road reserve widths and design

## Source

All standards are drawn from Section PE of [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] ("Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor, 2025"), published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]].
