# Vehicle Parking Standards

## Overview

Vehicle parking standards are one of the most consistently repeated technical requirements across Selangor's planning guidelines. [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] defines development-specific parking ratios across nearly every commercial chapter (PN1–PN20), while [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] provides the underlying technical design standards (TLK1–TLK11) — bay dimensions, angle configurations, aisle widths, and siting criteria — that apply across residential, commercial, and industrial contexts state-wide. [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] extends the same family of standards to community facilities (KM1–KM9) — hospitals, clinics, schools, tadika/taska, IPTA campuses, police and fire stations, mosques and surau, cemeteries, community centres, children's/elderly activity centres, libraries, public markets, and transport terminals — each with parking ratios and bay allocations calibrated to facility category and [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]]. Together these sources define a shared family of parking bay categories — general car parking, motorcycle parking, bus and lorry parking, bicycle parking, women's parking, e-hailing parking, ambulance parking, and disabled (OKU) parking — each sized differently depending on development type and vehicle class, and typically expressed as a ratio to floor area, unit count, or seating/room capacity.

## Core Parking Bay Categories

- **Car Parking (TLK1)** — the baseline requirement, generally calculated per unit of floor area (e.g. 1 bay per 46.45 sqm for terrace shops and hospitals) or per unit count (e.g. 1 bay per 3 hotel rooms). Standard bay dimensions are 2.5m x 5m, with [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] specifying detailed layouts for parallel (selari, 180°) and angled configurations (30°, 45°, 60°, 90°), including curb radius and one-way/two-way aisle widths for each angle. Community facilities in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] follow the same baseline but scale car park counts to a facility's population catchment tier — from IPTA campuses, state hospitals, and integrated transport terminals down to tadika/taska, small suraus, and pondok polis. IPTA campuses further split car parking into separate ratios by building function (academic/faculty, administration, residential colleges, sports complex, stadium, and support facilities such as clinics and libraries).
- **Tandem and Mechanical Parking (TLK2–TLK3)** — for space-constrained sites, combined/tandem bays (min. 2.5m x 5.0m per bay, max two bays per unit, in tandem, side-by-side, or linear arrangements) and automated mechanical stacking systems (bay 2.65m x 5.10m, accommodating 6–12 stacked cars with minimum lift/sling clearances) provide higher-density parking for strata and high-density commercial developments.
- **Motorcycle Parking (TLM/TLK8)** — usually set as a percentage of the car parking requirement (commonly 10–20%), reflecting motorcycle usage in commercial areas; the same 10–20% pattern recurs throughout [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] — e.g. 20% of car bays at public transport terminals and non-Islamic places of worship — and is also specified independently for hospitals, clinics, schools, tadika/taska, IPTA campuses, police and fire stations, suraus, and children's/elderly activity centres (PAKK/PAWE/PPDK). [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] sets minimum bay sizes of 1.0m x 2.0m (standard) and 1.2m x 2.5m (high-powered motorcycles, reserved at 10% of total motorcycle bays), with 45° or 90° angled layouts and clustered placement near building entrances.
- **Bus and Lorry Parking (TLK9–TLK10)** — standards not covered in the commercial development chapters but set out in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]]: bus bays sized 3.00m x 12.00m (mini bus 3.00m x 7.50m) with parallel or angled layouts and turning radius requirements; lorry bays scaled by vehicle class — small lorry (3m x 6m), big lorry (4m x 15m), and trailer (4m x 18m) — with headroom, unloading bay, and axle-count considerations. [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] applies bus bays to public transport terminals (KM9) and cemetery/crematorium visitor access (KM5), and dedicated lorry loading bays (minimum 3 bays, 9.8ft x 9.8ft) to public markets (KM8).
- **Bicycle Parking (TLK11)** — a minimum plot size of 0.70m x 1.83m (min. 9 sqm total area) with a 1.50m access pathway, rack spacing, roofing, and signage requirements; commercial guidelines permit up to 5% of standard car bays to be converted to bicycle parking. Bicycle parking also appears as a required or recommended component in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] — alongside e-scooter lanes at IPTA campuses, and as part of vehicle parking provision for primary schools (KM2-1) and integrated school complexes (KM2-3).
- **Women's Parking (TLKW/TLK6)** — a fixed allocation (typically 2% of total car bays) reserved for women, hotels, and sports facilities, positioned near entrances, CCTV/security points, or guarded areas, and sited in open, unobstructed locations away from isolated or hidden spots, per [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]].
- **E-hailing Bays** — dedicated pick-up/drop-off provisions for both cars and motorcycles, reflecting the growth of ride-hailing services; required across shop, complex, hotel, and institutional developments, and echoed in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] as lay-by and e-hailing pick-up/drop-off zones at clinics (minimum 5 bays), schools, and transport terminals.
- **Ambulance Parking** — a facility-specific bay category introduced prominently in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]]: hospitals require ambulance parking ratios that scale with hospital tier, health clinics require a minimum of 2 ambulance bays (with separate mother/ambulance bay allowances), and public transport terminals require a minimum of 2 ambulance bays as part of first-aid/emergency provision.
- **Disabled/OKU Parking (TLK5)** — a mandatory quota of total car bays (minimum 1 unit), positioned near lifts/entrances with ramps and railings. [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] specifies a minimum bay of 5.40m x 3.60m with a 1.20m wheelchair transfer zone, wheelchair symbol marking, and step-ramp connections (min. 1.00m width) to building entrances. The 2% quota recurs consistently across [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] facility types — libraries, markets, transport terminals, surau, places of worship, and community/elderly/children's activity centres — though some facilities (e.g. clinics, at 1 per 50–100 bays) scale OKU bays per fixed bay-count bands instead of a flat percentage. See [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] for the fuller accessibility framework this sits within.

## Variation by Development Type

While the categories are consistent, ratios scale with the intensity and nature of use:

- **Shophouses/shop-offices (PN1–PN3)**: 1 car bay per 46.45–92.90 sqm, plus visitor bay allowances for affordable units (20% additional).
- **Commercial complexes and MICE centres (PN4–PN5)**: parking scaled to gross floor area tiers, with formal TLKW and OKU quotas.
- **Hotels (PN6)**: distinct bus parking requirement (1 per 100 rooms) in addition to car parking tied to room count.
- **Hospitals (PN14; KM1-1)**: includes a unique requirement for dedicated ambulance parking (minimum 1 bay in PN14; expanded staff, visitor, patient, motorcycle, OKU, EV, and ambulance ratios by hospital tier — plus staff quarters allocations — in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]]).
- **Health clinics (KM1-2)**: parking ratios tied to clinic type (Jenis 1–7), with EV bays at 1 per 100 parking bays, OKU bays at 1 per 50–100 bays, minimum 2 ambulance bays, and minimum 5 lay-by/taxi/e-hailing bays.
- **Schools, tadika/taska, and IPTA campuses (KM2)**: primary and secondary schools provide car, motorcycle, bicycle, OKU, and EV parking sized to school tier, with reduced requirements for vertical/high-rise school models; tadika/taska set motorcycle and car parking ratios plus visitor parking; IPTA campuses scale parking separately by building component — academic/faculty, administration, residential colleges, sports complex, stadium, and support facilities — with added motorcycle, EV, and OKU allocations.
- **Police and fire stations (KM3)**: police headquarters, stations, and posts set quarters, staff, motorcycle, and OKU parking ratios tied to floor area or car-bay counts, with EV charging recommended at 1 bay per petak (lot); fire and rescue stations additionally require dedicated parking for fire trucks and quarters, plus motorcycle and OKU bays, with EV charging bay provision set at 1 lot per EV charging station.
- **Places of worship (KM4)**: parking tied to prayer-hall/musalla area for mosques, with suraus setting motorcycle and OKU parking (2% of total) against a smaller catchment; non-Islamic places of worship (RISI) set parking to gross floor area (1 car bay per 46.45 sqm, minimum 929.03 sqm total, plus 20% motorcycle and 2% OKU parking).
- **Cemeteries, crematoria, and columbaria (KM5)**: parking sized alongside disabled and motorcycle bays plus bus bays for visitor access.
- **Community centres, activity centres, and libraries (KM6–KM7)**: community complexes/centres scale parking (including OKU bays) by facility tier (Kompleks/Pusat Komuniti Jenis 1–2); children's and elderly activity centres and community-based rehabilitation centres (PAKK/PAWE/PPDK) set car, motorcycle, bicycle, and OKU parking against a fixed population catchment; public libraries (Jenis A–E) scale parking by library tier, with OKU parking fixed at 2% of total library parking.
- **Public markets (KM8)**: 1 car bay per 46.40 sqm gross floor area, 1 motorcycle bay per 92.90 sqm, OKU parking as a 2% addition to car bays, plus dedicated lorry loading bays.
- **Public transport terminals (KM9)**: parking scaled by terminal tier (Integrated, District, Hub, Support/Feeder, Bus Stop/Shelter), with motorcycle parking at 20% of car bays, OKU parking at 2%, and a minimum of 2 ambulance bays.
- **Golf courses and clubhouses (PN18–PN19)**: parking calculated via a composite formula spanning the golf course, clubhouse, driving range, staff quarters, and maintenance buildings.
- **Sports complexes (PN20)**: parking tied to seating capacity or floor area, with added bus parking for spectator events.
- **Ground-level parking design**: across development types, [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] applies CPTED-based safety criteria (TLK4) — avoiding blind spots, providing adequate lighting, CCTV, and signage, and using parking as a buffer zone between pedestrian paths and vehicle circulation. The same CPTED-informed siting logic (visibility, natural surveillance, guard posts) governs parking layout for safety and community facilities in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], including at police stations under [[entities/polis-diraja-malaysia]] and fire stations under [[entities/jabatan-bomba-dan-penyelamat]].

## Relationship to Other Standards

Parking provision is closely coupled with several other recurring requirements across Selangor's planning guidelines:

- **[[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]]** — EV charging bays are consistently defined as a percentage carve-out (typically 1%, minimum 1 unit) of the total parking bay count, situated near OKU bays; [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] applies the same 1-per-100-bay logic at health clinics and sets a 1-bay-per-lot recommendation at police stations and a 1-lot-per-EV-charging-station requirement at fire stations. [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] (TLK7) adds detailed technical requirements: charger classification (AC/DC, Slow/Fast/Rapid/Super Charging by power capacity), permissible locations (residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, petrol stations, R&R areas), minimum fire-separation distances from non-EV bays and wet/dry risers, sprinkler and detection requirements in basements, and provision of Vehicle Fire Blankets.
- **[[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]]** — disabled parking quotas are one component of the broader MS1184-compliant accessibility requirements (see [[entities/ms1184]]) that also cover ramps, walkways, and building access; this framework applies uniformly across commercial, residential, and community facility guidelines, from libraries and markets to activity centres for the elderly and persons with disabilities coordinated with [[entities/jabatan-kebajikan-masyarakat]].
- **[[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]]** — minimum road and lane widths adjoining parking areas (service roads, back lanes, driveway widths for large lots) are set jointly with road hierarchy standards in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]], and mirrored in facility-specific internal/service road widths (e.g. 15.24m internal, 20.12m service roads) throughout [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]].

## Source and Scope

These standards are drawn from [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (development-specific ratios), [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] (technical bay design, dimensions, and siting standards, TLK1–TLK11), and [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] (community facility parking standards, KM1–KM9), all published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] as part of the 2025 Selangor planning standards. Comparable parking bay frameworks (car, motorcycle, OKU, e-hailing) also appear in Selangor's industrial and residential planning guidelines — see [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] and [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] — indicating a shared, state-wide approach to vehicle parking provision across development sectors, unified under [[concepts/parking-standards]].
