# Geological Site Clearance (BPGT)

**Borang Pengesahan Geologi Tapak (BPGT)** is a geological site suitability verification form issued by the [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]] (Department of Minerals and Geoscience, JMG). It is required as a precondition for siting community facilities on land that falls within or near Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Kawasan Sensitif Alam Sekitar, KSAS) Class III/IV, former mining land, swamp/soft soil areas, or other geologically unstable terrain.

## Role in Community Facility Planning

Across [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], BPGT clearance recurs as a standard precondition wherever a facility site is proposed on or near geologically sensitive land. It functions as a risk-mitigation gate in the site selection process, applied in addition to — not instead of — other siting criteria such as catchment population, minimum site area, and road access.

## Facility Types Requiring BPGT (per PEQ-K-Masyarakat)

- **KM2 Pendidikan** — Primary schools (KM2-1) require geotechnical clearance via a Site Geological Clearance Form from JMG as part of site provision criteria (alongside drainage, buffer from industrial/pollution sources, and utility access); secondary schools (KM2-2) require BPGT/JMG geological safety assessment, particularly where slope stability is a concern; integrated school complexes (KM2-3) require a BPGT (Geology Suitability Report) from JMG as part of site selection criteria.
- **KM2-4 Tadika dan Taska** — Sites outside KSAS still require BPGT verification; siting is prohibited near quarries, abandoned mining pools, tailings dams, or contaminated land.
- **KM3 Kemudahan Keselamatan** — Police headquarters (IPK/IPD), police stations/posts, and fire and rescue stations (KM3-3) all require BPGT verification for Class III/IV land or peat areas, alongside avoidance of ex-mining or swamp land; fire stations additionally require geotechnical reports and Tapak Geology Approval Forms for Class III/IV land per the JBPM Fire Risk Analysis Grid Map process.
- **KM4 Kemudahan Keagamaan** — Mosques (KM4-1) require BPGT geological clearance for setback from mining/quarry areas involving KSAS Class III/IV or peat/soft soil zones; surau (KM4-2) require BPGT clearance for siting outside KSAS, with an outright prohibition on building on former mining land, landslide-prone areas, or wetlands without it.
- **KM5 Kemudahan Perkuburan** — Cemetery and burial land siting requires geotechnical/BPGT approval as part of broader site suitability checks (porous soil, flood-level clearance, groundwater protection).

Health facilities (KM1), community facilities (KM6), libraries (KM7), public markets (KM8), and transport terminals (KM9) in this document do not reference BPGT as a siting precondition, suggesting the requirement is concentrated on facility types most likely to be sited on marginal, hillside, or ex-mining land.

## Relationship to Other Siting Criteria

BPGT clearance typically operates alongside:

- **[[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]]** — setback distances from electrical substations, high-tension lines, and hazardous land uses are assessed jointly with geological risk.
- **[[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]]** — site area and hierarchy thresholds determine where a facility sits before geological suitability is confirmed for the specific parcel.

## Governing Authority

The [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]] is the sole issuing authority for BPGT approval, making early coordination with JMG a recurring recommendation throughout [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] for developers proposing community facilities on land of uncertain geological standing.
