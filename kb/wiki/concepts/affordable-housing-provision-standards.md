# Affordable Housing Provision Standards

Affordable housing provision is a core regulatory mechanism in [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]], operationalized through zoning-based quotas, fixed unit specifications, and pricing controls under the **Rumah Selangorku 3.0** framework. It ensures that housing supply matches residents' purchasing power across Selangor's varied urban and rural contexts.

## Zoning Framework

The state is divided into four zones of decreasing density and increasing rurality:

- **Zon 1** — high-density urban areas
- **Zon 2** — urban areas
- **Zon 3** — urban fringe areas
- **Zon 4** — rural areas

Each zone is mapped to specific districts and mukim, with boundaries illustrated in the guideline's state map (Rajah PR(1)).

## Mandatory Quotas (Jadual PR(2))

Developers must provide a minimum percentage of affordable units, with the required percentage varying by:

- **Zone** (Zon 1–4)
- **Land size** of the development (from under 5 acres to over 50 acres)
- **Location** — developments within Transit-Oriented Development (TOD), Transit Adjacent Development (TAD), or Central Business District (CBD) areas face different (generally higher) quotas than non-TOD areas
- **Housing type** — landed housing (Solo/Duo/Trio/Quad) versus strata housing carries distinct quota structures

This quota system directly complements [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] policy, since TOD-adjacent land is treated as a priority zone for affordable housing delivery, reinforcing the goal of housing access near transit infrastructure.

## Component Standards (Jadual PR(3))

Rumah Selangorku 3.0 defines fixed product specifications for affordable units:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Solo | Smallest single-unit format |
| Duo | Two-unit paired format |
| Trio | Three-unit format |
| Quad/Teres | Four-unit terrace format |

Each type has a minimum built-up area, a fixed number of bedrooms, and a **fixed selling price ranging from RM114,750 to RM250,000**, ensuring affordability is locked in at the point of sale rather than left to market pricing.

## Technical Planning Standards (PR7)

Beyond pricing and quotas, [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]]'s Bahagian B (PR7: Rumah Selangorku) sets detailed physical planning standards mirroring those for market-rate housing (PR1–PR6), but calibrated to affordable-housing density and cost targets. These cover:

- Lot size, floor area, bedroom count, storey height, and block length for terrace/town house formats
- Plinth area, density, plot coverage, and podium levels for apartment formats
- Parking ratios (car, motorcycle, OKU, EV charging bays)
- Communal facilities: surau, funeral management space, halls, mailboxes, management office, childcare (Tadika/Taska), and elderly activity spaces sized by unit count

These requirements reflect the same inclusive design commitments seen across the guideline — see [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]] for how OKU, elderly, and childcare provisions are standardized across housing types.

## Policy Alignment

The affordable housing provision framework is not standalone; it operationalizes higher-level policy commitments:

- **[[entities/dpn2]]** (2nd National Housing Policy) sets national objectives for adequate, quality, affordable home ownership and rental housing, including TOD housing and multi-generation housing strategies.
- **[[entities/rmk12]]** (12th Malaysia Plan) identifies housing supply and affordability as one of five national housing sector focus areas.
- **[[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]]** (State Structure Plan) embeds housing type/cost and ownership targets within its Hala Tuju Strategik (MM1–MM5).
- **[[entities/rfn4]]** (4th National Physical Plan) frames affordable housing within broader spatial and livable-neighborhood planning.
- **[[entities/bandar-berdaya-huni-negeri-selangor]]** (Livable City Selangor framework) treats affordable housing as foundational to its Livability pillar.

## Governance

The standards are issued and enforced by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] in coordination with the 12 local authorities of Selangor, ensuring the quota and pricing framework is applied uniformly across planning approvals statewide. Strata-titled affordable schemes must additionally comply with the [[entities/strata-titles-act-1985]] (Act 318) for parcel and common property arrangements.
