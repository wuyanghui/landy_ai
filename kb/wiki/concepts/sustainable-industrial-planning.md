# Sustainable Industrial Planning

## Overview

Sustainable Industrial Planning is a cross-cutting theme in [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]], the Selangor State Industrial Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025. Rather than being confined to a single section, it threads through the policy framework, best-practice initiatives, and general planning standards that govern how industrial land is developed across the state.

## Policy Foundation

The guideline grounds sustainable industrial development in a stack of national and state policy instruments:

- Seven UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17), covering economic growth, industry/infrastructure, sustainable cities, responsible production, climate action, life on land, and partnerships
- [[entities/rmk12]] (12th Malaysia Plan), which emphasizes eco-industrial parks, green/clean industry practices, and R&D support for high-value manufacturing
- [[entities/rfn4]] (Fourth National Physical Plan), focused on dynamic and balanced industrial growth management
- [[entities/nimp2030]] (New Industrial Master Plan 2030), whose four missions include a net-zero carbon emission pathway alongside economic complexity, technology adoption, and economic inclusivity
- [[entities/dpn2]] (Second National Urbanisation Policy), promoting efficient and renewable energy use in industrial areas (solar farms, biogas)
- [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] (Selangor Structural Plan 2035), addressing water catchment protection alongside industrial land use
- [[entities/netr]] (National Energy Transition Roadmap), setting Malaysia's renewable energy targets (31% by 2025, 40% by 2035, 70% by 2050) via six strategic levers: Energy Efficiency, Renewable Energy, Hydrogen, Bioenergy, Green Mobility, and Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage

## Eco-Industrial Park (EIP) Best Practices

Section 4.0 of the guideline introduces the Eco-Industrial Parks (EIP) concept, listing seven best-practice initiatives for green industrial development:

1. **Kitar Semula/Guna Semula** — recycling and reuse of materials
2. **Reka Bentuk Hijau/Landskap/Ruang Terbuka** — green design, landscaping, and open spaces
3. **Kecekapan Tenaga** — energy efficiency, illustrated through rooftop solar panel installations
4. **Pencegahan Pencemaran** — pollution prevention via dedicated waste management zones
5. **Pengeluaran Bersih** — clean production complying with SIRIM standards
6. **Hubungan Antara Syarikat** — inter-company collaboration and industrial symbiosis
7. **Konsep Ekologi** — conservation of local natural resources

These are illustrated in the guideline with real-world examples: an ethanol plant reusing industrial waste, Ecobusiness Park V in Punggol as a green building showcase, rooftop solar panel systems, and rainwater harvesting (SPAH) integrated into landscaping.

## Site-Level Sustainability Principles

Sustainability is also embedded in the eight foundational site-planning principles set out in the guideline, which govern how industrial land is zoned and designed:

- **Perancangan Tapak** (site planning) — zoning by pollution level, site suitability, and safety measures (flood, geotechnical, water catchment)
- **Berkomponen dan Berkualiti** — quality development complying with ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) principles and IoT technology adoption
- **Zon Penampan** (buffer zones) — separation distances recommended by the Department of Environment, connecting directly to [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]]

## Relationship to Worker Welfare

Sustainable planning is not limited to environmental measures — the guideline treats social sustainability (adequate housing, canteens, and amenities for industrial workers) as an equally weighted challenge, addressed under [[concepts/worker-welfare-facilities]].

## Significance

By anchoring industrial development to SDGs, national economic plans, and energy transition targets, and by codifying EIP best practices into formal planning guidance, the document positions sustainability as a baseline expectation rather than an optional add-on for industrial developments in Selangor.
