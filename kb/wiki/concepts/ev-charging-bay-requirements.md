# Electric Vehicle Charging Bay (EVCB) Requirements

Electric Vehicle Charging Bays (EVCB) are a mandatory support facility across Selangor's development planning framework, addressed as a percentage-based parking quota in [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (the Selangor State Business Planning Guidelines 2025), as a detailed technical standard (TLK7: Petak Pengecasan Kenderaan Elektrik) in [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] (the Selangor State Infrastructure Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025), and as a recurring line-item ratio embedded within individual facility standards in [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] (the Selangor State Community Facilities Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025). All three documents are published by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]], and together they reflect the state's alignment with Malaysia's broader energy transition and smart-city policy direction referenced in each guideline's policy framework.

## Core Standard

Across the twenty specific-use chapters (PN1–PN20) of PEQ-Perniagaan — including terrace shops and shop-offices, affordable terrace shops, semi-detached/detached shops, commercial complexes and offices, MICE centres, hotels, hypermarkets/departmental stores, service stations, bazaars and arcades, rest and service areas (RnR), hawker centres, SOHO, service apartments, private hospitals, private schools, private higher education institutions, theme parks, golf courses, clubhouses, and sports complexes — the recurring baseline requirement is:

- **Minimum 1% of total car parking bays** allocated as EVCB, or
- **A minimum of one (1) unit**, whichever development has fewer bays

Some larger-scale residential-adjacent typologies (e.g., service apartments) specify a higher floor, up to 5% of bays scaled by unit count, reflecting greater expected EV uptake in long-stay residential-commercial developments.

PEQ-K-Masyarakat applies a closely aligned ratio within its own facility-specific parking tables rather than as a standalone chapter: KM1-2 (Klinik Kesihatan) sets **1 EV parking bay per 100 parking bays** across all seven health clinic tiers, matching the 1% baseline found in PEQ-Perniagaan. Other KM chapters — including KM1-1 (Hospital), KM2-5 (IPTA), KM3-1/KM3-2 (police headquarters, stations, and posts), and KM9 (public transport terminals) — likewise list EV parking as a fixed allocation alongside OKU and motorcycle bays, though the summarized standard does not always specify the exact ratio, instead scaling it to each facility's overall car park provision the same way PEQ-Perniagaan does.

PEQ-infrastruktur's TLK7 standard extends this quota logic state-wide: it sets EVCB quantity requirements for strata and landed developments (both residential and non-residential), including proportional quotas for OKU/disabled EV bays and motorcycle EV bays, plus "EV-Ready" provisioning (electrical infrastructure pre-installed for future charger fitting even where a physical charger is not yet required). It also distinguishes between **Sedia Ada** (existing) and **Baharu** (new) development types, with qualifying criteria tied to CCC/CFO status and KM/PK (Kebenaran Merancang/Pelan Kebenaran) submission stage — meaning the applicable EVCB obligation depends on how far a project has progressed through approvals at the point the standard takes effect.

## Charging Equipment Types

PEQ-infrastruktur classifies EV charging equipment by current type and power delivery: AC and DC charging, spanning Slow, Fast, Rapid, and Super Charging categories, each defined by power capacity (kW) and expected charging duration. This classification underpins the siting and fire-safety separation standards below, since higher-power DC rapid/super chargers carry different risk and clearance requirements than slow AC units.

## Siting and Design Requirements

EVCB placement is consistently linked to [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] provisions: bays are required to be sited near OKU (disabled) parking areas and designed to remain motorcycle-friendly where mixed-use parking areas apply. This co-location reflects the guideline's broader principle of clustering accessibility- and technology-enabled facilities near building entrances and lift lobbies for ease of access. PEQ-K-Masyarakat reinforces this same co-location pattern at the facility level — EV bays appear consistently paired with OKU parking line items across health, safety, education, and transport terminal standards, rather than being treated as an independently sited element.

PEQ-infrastruktur adds detail on permissible EVCB locations — residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional developments, petrol stations, and rest and service areas (R&R) — and siting criteria covering outdoor versus indoor placement, installation at building roof level, open/unenclosed rooftop parking, and mandatory distance from fire escape routes. Required support elements include CCTV, waiting areas, signage, solar-powered systems, adequate lighting, wheel stops and bumpers, and digital information/payment apps, alongside sizing standards specific to cars and motorcycles.

## Fire Safety and Separation Standards

Because EV charging introduces distinct fire-risk considerations, PEQ-infrastruktur sets out minimum separation and fire-separation distances between EVCB and adjacent non-EV parking lots, differentiated by outdoor/rooftop versus indoor location and by AC versus DC charging, including maximum floor area per fire compartment and required fire wall height/rating — standards consistent with the enforcement role of Jabatan Bomba dan Penyelamat. Additional requirements cover minimum distance from wet riser, dry riser, or fire hydrant points; automatic fire sprinkler and detection systems for EVCB located in basements or multi-storey parking structures; placement and clearance of main electrical isolation switches relative to EVCB and any shared isolation switches; and provision of Vehicle Fire Blankets (VFB) scaled to the number of EVCB units installed. These technical safeguards apply as a baseline across development types and complement — rather than replace — the sector-specific quota rules in PEQ-Perniagaan and PEQ-K-Masyarakat.

## Relationship to Parking Standards

EVCB quotas are calculated as a percentage of the total parking bay count established under each chapter's [[concepts/parking-standards]] (car, motorcycle, women's, and e-hailing bay ratios), rather than as an independent standalone allocation. This means EVCB requirements scale automatically with a development's size, floor area, and use intensity — larger hypermarkets, hotels, and sports complexes yield proportionally larger EVCB provision even though the percentage rate stays constant. PEQ-K-Masyarakat follows the same logic at the facility level: a Klinik Komuniti serving under 5,000 residents receives proportionally fewer EV bays than a Hospital Negeri serving over 1.5 million, since both derive from the same fixed ratio applied to a very different base parking count.

## Notable Variations

- **Golf courses (PN18)** and **clubhouses (PN19)** specify EVCB ratios tied to a formula combining floor areas of multiple components (golf course, clubhouse, driving range, staff quarters, maintenance buildings), rather than a single parking pool.
- **Terrace shop-offices and affordable terrace shops (PN1–PN3)** apply the 1% baseline uniformly regardless of block length or storey count.
- **Sports complexes (PN20)** tie EVCB counts to both total parking supply and OKU parking allocation, given the higher expected accessible-parking demand at spectator venues.
- **Strata and landed residential developments**, addressed only in PEQ-infrastruktur's TLK7 (not the PN1–PN20 commercial chapters), follow a separate quota structure that folds in OKU and motorcycle EV bay allocations alongside EV-Ready pre-wiring requirements.
- **Fire and rescue stations (KM3-3)** in PEQ-K-Masyarakat depart from the percentage-of-parking model entirely, instead specifying a minimum land allocation of **1 lot per EV charging station** — a site-area guidance rather than a ratio of existing car park bays, reflecting the operational-vehicle-dominant nature of fire station parking.
- **Health clinics (KM1-2)** apply a clean 1-per-100 ratio uniform across all seven clinic tiers (Jenis 1–7), the most explicitly stated fixed EVCB ratio found in PEQ-K-Masyarakat.

## Significance

The near-universal inclusion of EVCB standards across all twenty commercial development types in PEQ-Perniagaan, reinforced by a dedicated cross-sectoral technical standard (TLK7) in PEQ-infrastruktur covering equipment classification, siting, and fire safety, and further echoed as a recurring line-item across nearly every facility category (health, safety, education, and transport) in PEQ-K-Masyarakat, signals that PLANMalaysia Selangor treats EV charging infrastructure as a baseline utility requirement — comparable to fire safety or prayer room provision — rather than an optional green-building enhancement. This consistency across three separately published guideline documents marks a deliberate, state-wide policy shift from earlier editions of the guidelines (e.g., the 2022 editions referenced in both PEQ-infrastruktur's Tujuan section and PEQ-K-Masyarakat's Prakata).
