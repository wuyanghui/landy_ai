# Burial and Cemetery Planning Standards

Burial and cemetery planning is addressed in **KM5: Kemudahan Perkuburan** of [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]], the most extensive facility chapter in the guideline. It sets out how burial land is sited, sized, and managed across Selangor's religious communities, and introduces mechanisms for reusing constrained burial land and funding future provision.

## Religious Differentiation in Site Selection

Cemetery siting criteria differ by faith community:

- **Islamic burial land** — the majority allocation (60% of combined burial land under the population-based sizing formula), oriented so graves face the Qiblat.
- **Chinese cemeteries** — favor hillside sites reflecting feng shui principles.
- **Hindu/Sikh cemeteries** — incorporate ancestor-worship elements; Hindu graves also face the Qiblat per the guideline's siting convention.
- **Christian and other-religion cemeteries** — separate siting provisions, alongside general/multi-faith burial land and integrated cemetery complexes that combine several religious sections on one site.

## Site Suitability Criteria

Common technical requirements apply regardless of religion:

- Porous soil suited to burial use.
- Avoidance of flood-prone land — sites must sit at least 2m below the 100-year ARI flood level.
- Assessment of topography, soil structure, and groundwater protection, including minimum distances from water sources.
- Mandatory geotechnical clearance, following the same [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] process (BPGT approval from [[entities/jabatan-mineral-dan-geosains-jmg]]) used across other KM facility categories for KSAS Class III/IV and peat/soft-soil land.

## Land Sizing and [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]]

Cemetery land needs follow a tiered hierarchy from neighbourhood to district level, with acreage apportioned 60% Islamic / 40% other religions. Formulas convert projected death rates and standard burial-plot dimensions into required land area, and standard grave/plot dimensions, row spacing, and orientation are specified for consistent layout.

## Site Infrastructure

Cemetery layouts must provide circulation via service and internal roads, landscaped [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]] (minimum 1.5m buffer), parking including disabled and motorcycle bays, bus bays, and public amenities such as toilets and rest areas.

## Perkuburan Bertingkat (Tiered/Multi-Level Burial)

To address land scarcity in fully-used cemeteries, the guideline permits **tiered burial** — reusing sites through up to three vertical burial levels at 1.22m height increments. Three illustrated methods are provided, covering land reclamation/embankment approaches and bucket-ripping/topsoil-management techniques for preparing each successive layer.

## Crematoria and Columbaria

- **Crematorium standards**: minimum 0.6 hectare site, defined setback/buffer distances, modern gas-fired crematorium equipment, and building components including halls, offices, storage, and changing rooms, plus parking and perimeter planting. Krematorium Shah Alam is cited as a reference example.
- **Columbarium (Kolumbarium) standards**: minimum 2-acre site, at least 1,000m separation from residential areas, defined land-use zoning, and building components covering ash storage, a chapel, and waiting rooms. Kolumbarium Semenyih is cited as a reference example.

## Funding Mechanism

The [[entities/tabung-amanah-perkuburan-negeri-selangor]] (Selangor State Cemetery Trust Fund) requires developer contributions from housing projects meeting a threshold (≥10,000 population or ≥250 acres), with contribution rates of RM500 or RM300 per plot depending on district, and includes burial-fund/kubur bayaran mechanisms with racial quota considerations (Rizab Melayu).
