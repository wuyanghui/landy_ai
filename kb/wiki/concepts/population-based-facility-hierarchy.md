# Population-Based Facility Hierarchy

Population-based facility hierarchy is the organizing principle underlying nearly all standards in the [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] guideline (Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025). Rather than applying a single fixed standard, each community facility category is tiered into multiple classes, with minimum site area, building height/storeys, service radius, and supporting infrastructure scaled to the size of the population it serves — from village/kampung level up to Bandar Global/Wilayah (metropolitan) level.

## How the Hierarchy Works

Across KM1–KM9, facilities are typically classified using an urban hierarchy tied to settlement type:

- **Bandar Global/Wilayah** — largest tier, populations often exceeding 1.5 million
- **Bandar Negeri** — state-level cities, ~100,001–1.49 million
- **Bandar Utama** — major towns, >100,000
- **Bandar Tempatan** — local towns, 20,000–100,000
- **Pekan/Kampung** — small towns and villages, lowest catchment tier

As catchment population rises, so does the required site area, building capacity, road width, and range of supporting facilities. For several categories — hospitals, schools, and community complexes — higher catchment tiers are also paired with a choice between conventional low-rise (4 storeys and below) and vertical/high-rise (5 storeys and above) development models, allowing the same population-driven capacity to be met on a smaller footprint in land-constrained urban areas.

## Examples Across Facility Categories

- **Health (KM1):** Hospital tiers (Institusi Perubatan Khas, Hospital Negeri, Hospital Pakar Major/Minor, Hospital Tanpa Pakar) are matched to city hierarchy and bed counts (>300 beds down to <150 beds). Klinik Kesihatan is split into 7 types (Jenis 1–7) by 5km-radius catchment population, from >30,001 residents down to <5,000; Klinik Komuniti serves under 5,000 residents, typically sited in shophouse lots.
- **Education (KM2):** Primary schools serve 5,000–8,000 residents per school (min 6–max 36 classrooms); secondary schools serve 9,000–12,000; integrated school complexes (combined primary + secondary) serve 13,000–15,000 on a minimum 13-acre site; tadika/taska serve 200–400 housing units per unit; IPTA campuses require a minimum site of over 50 acres, sized to catchment population and vertical/compact design choices.
- **Safety (KM3):** Police hierarchy runs from Ibu Pejabat Polis Kontijen (state-level, 15 acres) and Ibu Pejabat Polis Daerah (district-level, 10 acres) down through Balai Polis (15,000–40,000 population, 5–10 acres) to Pondok Polis (5,000–10,000 population, 2.5 acres). Fire stations use a 5-category system (A–E) from global/main city (>100,000 population) down to community/village level, with minimum 75km spacing between stations.
- **Religious (KM4):** Mosques scale from Masjid Negeri (state capital, 10 ekar) through Masjid Daerah and Masjid Tempatan (local settlement, 3 ekar); surau serve as few as 500 housing units (2,000 residents); non-Islamic places of worship (Rumah Ibadat Selain Islam) serve a 5,000-resident/1,250-unit catchment on a minimum 1.5-acre site.
- **Community (KM6):** Community complexes/centres are split into four types (Kompleks Komuniti Jenis 1 & 2, Pusat Komuniti Jenis 1 & 2) by catchment (2,001–15,000 people), each tied to urban hierarchy (Bandar Global down to Bandar/Pekan); children's/elderly activity centres (PAKK/PAWE) and community rehabilitation centres (PPDK) serve a shared 10,000-resident catchment; public libraries use a five-tier system (Jenis A–E) from state/national (4-acre sites) down to village level (under 1 acre).
- **Open space (KM7):** A hierarchy from Taman Wilayah/Taman Negeri down through Taman Bandar, Taman Tempatan, Padang Kejiranan, Padang Permainan/Laman Sukan, to Lot Permainan, each with area, population served, and travel-distance standards.
- **Markets (KM8):** Pasar Awam is triggered at a 2,500-house/10,000-resident threshold, with a minimum 1.2-acre site.
- **Transport (KM9):** Terminal types range from Integrated Terminal (50 acres) through District Terminal (25 acres) and Public Transport Hub Terminal (12.5 acres) to Support/Feeder Terminal (4 acres) down to bus stops/shelters.
- **Burial (KM5):** Cemetery land size follows a neighbourhood-to-district population hierarchy, with acreage split 60% Islamic / 40% other religions, and contribution rates to the Selangor State Cemetery Trust Fund scaled to development size (≥10,000 population or ≥250 acres triggers mandatory contribution).

## Purpose and Rationale

This tiered approach ensures facilities are proportionate to demand, preventing both under-provision in high-density areas and inefficient over-provision in sparsely populated ones — directly addressing the imbalanced distribution and mismatch between facility provision and population growth identified as key issues in the guideline's background section. It also supports consistency with higher-order planning instruments such as [[entities/rsn-selangor-2035]] and [[entities/rmk12]], and is applied alongside related siting principles such as [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]], [[concepts/parking-standards]], [[concepts/vertical-facility-development]], and [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]].

The hierarchy is issued and maintained by [[entities/planmalaysia-selangor]] and approved through MMKN (Mesyuarat Majlis Kerajaan Negeri).
