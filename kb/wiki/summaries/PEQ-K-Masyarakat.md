## 1.0 TUJUAN (pages 16–16)

Summary: This partial document (page 2, Bahagian A) covers Section 1.0 (Tujuan/Objectives) and Section 2.0 (Latar Belakang/Background) of Selangor's Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat 2025. Section 1.0 states the guideline's purpose as a reference for local authorities (PBN, PBT), technical agencies, and developers to ensure community facility planning is holistic, consistent, and integrated with GPP, RSN, RT, and RKK planning documents. Section 2.0 outlines the background, defining community facilities as basic infrastructure/services supporting daily well-being, and introduces Section 2.1, which lists four key issues and challenges in community facility development: (1) imbalanced facility distribution between high-density and less-populated areas, (2) inadequate/underutilized existing facilities not centrally or efficiently located, (3) mismatch between facility provision and population growth leading to inclusivity gaps, and (4) social issues (crime, unsafe access) linked to poorly planned, underutilized recreational spaces. It concludes that strategic, comprehensive planning is needed to ensure accessible, quality, sustainable facilities that support community welfare.

- **3.4 Rancangan Struktur Negeri Selangor 2035** – Three development thrusts (Efficient Resource Hub, Livable, Competitive), detailing Hala Tuju Strategik 2 (education/health/safety facilities, MM7–MM10) and Hala Tuju Strategik 3 (community, religious, recreation, sports facilities, MM11–MM14).
- **3.5 Dasar Perbandaran Negara 2 (DPN2)** – Urban policy principles including Prinsip 2 (Bandar Berdaya Huni) and Prinsip 4 (Inclusive & Equitable Urban Development), illustrated with Shah Alam cityscape and OKU (disabled)-friendly infrastructure examples.
- **3.6 Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED)** – Explanation of CPTED as a crime-prevention design approach with its 4 universal principles (Natural Surveillance, Access Control, Territorial Reinforcement, Maintenance & Management) and 3 implementation strategies, expanded into 5 numbered design elements (Natural Surveillance, Access Control, Territoriality, Maintenance & Management, Support Activity), with photographic examples of pedestrian walkways, public markets, and public spaces.

Overall, this part establishes the national and state-level policy foundations (SDGs, RMK-12, RFN4, Selangor Structure Plan 2035, DPN2, CPTED) that justify and guide the standards for planning community facilities in Selangor.

## 5.0 PRINSIP ASAS PERANCANGAN (pages 25–25)

Summary: This page (Section 5.0 "Prinsip Asas Perancangan" / Basic Planning Principles) from the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor 2025 outlines seven core principles governing the planning of community facilities: (1) Pengagihan Kemudahan Masyarakat Yang Saksama (equitable distribution balancing hierarchy of planning and residents' needs, distinguishing shared "Kemudahan Bersama" from gated/private "Kemudahan Awam"); (2) Kemudahsampaian (accessibility, requiring facilities be reachable within about 400m or a 5-minute walk from residential catchments, with connected pedestrian/cycling paths especially for OKU/disabled users); (3) Keselesaan dan Keselamatan (comfort and safety, referencing CPTED and Universal Design Guidelines, safe visual surveillance, and Temporary Placement Centres for disaster/pandemic use); (4) Interaksi Sosial (facilities designed to encourage harmonious social interaction reflecting local culture); (5) Integrasi Kemudahan (integration/consolidation of facilities into complex developments); (6) Struktur dan Karakter (built forms emphasizing aesthetics, sense of place, and adaptable/resilient landscape and architectural design for disaster/pandemic recovery); and (7) Teknologi (alignment with Industrial Revolution 4.0, smart and efficient digital infrastructure supporting resilient urban development).

### KM2-4 TADIKA DAN TASKA (pages 45–47)

Summary: This section (KM2-4: Tadika dan Taska) from the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor, 2025" sets out planning guidelines and standards for kindergarten (tadika) and nursery (taska) facilities, covering both landed and strata developments:

- **Catchment population**: 1 unit tadika/taska per 200–400 housing units.
- **Site area & catchment**: Minimum 0.25 acre (0.1011 hectare); maximum service radius of 400 meters (5–10 minutes' walk) from residential areas; maximum class size of 25 pupils/class.
- **Building size guidance (strata)**: Provides worked examples for calculating minimum floor area based on unit count (e.g., 200 units = 55.74 m², 250–400 units = 74.32 m², >400 units add 0.186 m² per additional unit), plus example calculations.
- **Location and site criteria**: Should be near community amenities (surau, community hall, recreational parks); away from major roads, industrial areas, high-voltage lines/pylons, and geologically unstable zones; strata facilities should be at ground floor/podium level with easy drop-off access.
- **Environmental controls**: Prohibits siting near quarries, abandoned mining pools, tailings dams, contaminated land, or flood-prone/fire-risk areas; landed sites require large open reserve land; strata buildings need dedicated ramps, loading bays, and pedestrian access separated from vehicle/logistics routes; also references KSAS (Kawasan Sensitif Alam Sekitar) restrictions and requirement for a Borang Pengesahan Geologi Tapak (BPGT) from JMG for sites outside KSAS.
- **Building setbacks and safety**: Minimum setbacks from front/side boundaries (6.10 m/20 ft); requirement for perimeter fencing, safety features, and restrictions on adjacency to industrial/hazardous facilities.
- **Facility components checklist**: Lists required rooms/spaces such as office, teaching rooms, sickbay/clinic, staff room, kitchen/pantry, signage, toilets, flagpoles (Malaysia, Selangor, and school flags), and fire safety equipment.
- **Parking and amenities**: Specifies motorcycle and car parking ratios, visitor parking, landscaping/open space requirements (minimum 5% of site area), and indoor/outdoor play area provisions (podium/roof garden for strata).
- **Notes/exceptions**: Clarifies rules on land use conversion, PBT approval requirements, and flexibility for large developments (≥800 units) to combine facility provision.
- **Illustrations**: Includes example photos and diagrams (Rajah KM2-4(1) and KM2-4(2)) showing a real tadika example (Tadika Big Apple USJ4) and an annotated perspective diagram illustrating key components like the main building, parking, play area, and service road.
