# Preface (pages 1–12)

Summary: This partial document comprises the front matter of "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Selangor State Industrial Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025), published by Jabatan Perancangan Bandar dan Desa Negeri Selangor (PLANMalaysia Selangor) in collaboration with 12 local authorities (MBSA, MBPJ, MBSJ, MBDK, MPS, MPKL, MPKS, MPAJ, MPHS, MPS Selayang, MPHS Hulu Selangor, MDSB). It covers: the cover page and copyright/publication details (ISBN, publisher address, copyright notice); a saving clause and disclaimer regarding the document's legal status and use of illustrations; a foreword (Prakata) explaining the guideline's purpose, its 2025 update history, approval by MMKN (State Executive Council) sittings, and the collaborating agencies/associations involved (PAM, LPBM, MIP, REHDA); a detailed table of contents (Kandungan) outlining Bahagian A (Scope and Development Direction, covering objectives, background, policies, definitions, best practices, general standards) and Bahagian B (Specific Planning Guidelines for industrial types such as terrace, semi-detached, clustered, strata, tanah bertingkat, data centres, worker accommodation, and Managed Industrial Park); a list of abbreviations (Singkatan) covering technical, regulatory, and infrastructure terms; a glossary (Glosari) defining key planning terms (e.g., Alam Sekitar, Anjakan Bangunan, Industri Berat/Sederhana/Ringan, Industri Strata, IoT, MIP, Skim Strata, Zon Penampan); and a list of legal authorities and references (Punca Kuasa/Peruntukan Perundangan) including Act 172, DPN2, RFN-4, RMK-12, RSN, and various PLANMalaysia guidelines that underpin the document.

## LATAR BELAKANG (pages 14–14)

Summary: This page (page 2 of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025") covers two sections: (1) **1.0 Tujuan (Objectives)** — explaining that the guideline was prepared to help PBT and PBN technical staff plan, implement, and control industrial development consistently, ensuring compliance with regulations, coordinated infrastructure provision, and environmental sustainability, and (2) **2.0 Latar Belakang (Background), 2.1 Isu dan Cabaran dalam Pembangunan Perindustrian (Issues and Challenges in Industrial Development)** — listing four key challenges: (1) conflicts between incompatible industrial zoning types located near each other; (2) imbalanced industrial land-use planning causing pollution and safety issues near residential areas; (3) inadequate site planning for waste and drainage management leading to environmental and health problems; and (4) insufficient worker welfare facilities (housing, canteens, cleanliness) within industrial areas.

## DASAR DAN POLISI PEMBANGUNAN (pages 15–19)

Summary: This section (Bahagian A, pages 3–7) outlines the policy and planning framework underpinning industrial planning guidelines and standards for Selangor. It covers: (1) the seven relevant UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17) and their targets for economic growth, industry/infrastructure, sustainable cities, responsible production, climate action, life on land, and partnerships; (2) the 12th Malaysia Plan (RMK12), highlighting Theme 1 on economic revitalisation, focus on high-value services/manufacturing/agriculture sectors, eco-industrial parks, green/clean industry practices, and support for MSMEs and R&D; (3) the Fourth National Physical Plan (RFN4), focusing on dynamic and balanced industrial growth management; (4) the New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030), detailing its priority and relevant industry sectors, sector-specific policies, four missions (economic complexity, technology adoption, net-zero carbon emission pathway, economic inclusivity), and Mission Based Projects; (5) the Second National Urbanisation Policy (DPN2) with objectives and strategies on affordable housing for industrial workers and efficient/renewable energy use (e.g., solar farms, biogas) in industrial areas; (6) the Selangor Structural Plan 2035 (RSN Selangor 2035), addressing water catchment protection, public transport, and industrial land use planning; and (7) the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), presenting Malaysia's renewable energy targets (31% by 2025, 40% by 2035, 70% by 2050) and six strategic energy levers (Energy Efficiency, Renewable Energy, Hydrogen, Bioenergy, Green Mobility, and Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage).

It also introduces Section 5.0 on climate adaptation in industrial planning, and includes four illustrative photo examples (Rajah IND(1)-(4)): an Ethanol Plant reusing industrial waste, Ecobusiness Park V in Punggol showcasing green building design, a solar panel installation on an industrial roof, and a rainwater harvesting (SPAH) system integrated into landscaping.

## PRINSIP ASAS PERANCANGAN DAN DEFINISI PERINDUSTRIAN (pages 21–22)

Summary: Bahagian ini (Bahagian A, ms. 9–10 dokumen "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025") membincangkan dua topik utama:

**5.0 Prinsip Asas Perancangan dan Definisi Perindustrian**

**5.1 Prinsip Asas Perancangan** — menyenaraikan 8 prinsip asas yang perlu dipatuhi dalam perancangan kawasan perindustrian:
1. Perancangan Tapak — pengezonan mengikut kadar pencemaran, kesesuaian lokasi, dan langkah keselamatan (banjir, geoteknikal, air tadahan)
2. Berkomponen dan Berkualiti — pembangunan berkualiti tinggi mematuhi prinsip ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) dan teknologi IoT
3. Kemudahsampaian — peletakan kawasan industri yang mudah diakses secara logistik
4. Keluasan dan Saiz Minimum — pengoptimuman saiz lot, landskap, ruang penyimpanan dan pemunggahan
5. Anjakan Bangunan — anjakan bagi keselamatan kebakaran dan air craft clearance
6. Kawalan Ketinggian — mengelakkan gangguan terhadap kawasan sekitar dan rekabentuk
7. Zon Penampan — mengikut cadangan Jabatan Alam Sekitar
8. Bersih dan Indah — landskap, penampilan kawasan, sistem lalu lintas dan ruang letak kenderaan

**5.2 Definisi Perindustrian** — merujuk definisi Kamus JPBD (Disember 2022), membahagikan industri kepada beberapa kategori dengan gambar rajah/foto contoh bagi setiap kategori:
1. **Industri Khas**, termasuk:
   - Industri Berteknologi Tinggi (contoh: Aerospace)
   - Industri Pembuatan Berteknologi Tinggi, Penyelidikan & Pembangunan Rekabentuk, Makmal Kajian, Pemprosesan Maklumat/Pusat Komputer, Pembangunan Perisian, Pusat Latihan Perindustrian, Pusat Pengagihan
2. **Industri Penerbangan (Aerospace)** — kawasan komposit menyediakan kemudahan komponen dan penyelenggaraan industri berhampiran lapangan terbang
3. **Industri Ringan** — pencemaran minimum, boleh ditempatkan berhampiran kawasan perumahan
4. **Industri Sederhana** — menghasilkan bahan buangan, bunyi, gas sederhana; perlu jarak dari kawasan perumahan
5. **Industri Berat** — menghasilkan bahan mentah dan buangan sederhana/tinggi, risiko kebakaran, letupan, pencemaran biologi

## PIAWAIAN UMUM (pages 23–28)

Summary: This section (Bahagian A, pages 11–16) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025" covers General Standards (Piawaian Umum) for industrial planning in Selangor, including:

- **Section 6.0 General Standards**: Core planning principles for industrial development referencing the Detailed Local Plan/Special Area Plan.
- **Plot ratio calculation**: Definition of Nisbah Plot (total floor area vs. plot land area), listing spaces excluded from GFA calculation (staircases, lifts, lobbies, toilets, parking, plant rooms, landscape decks, etc.), with reference to State Selangor Planning Committee Circular No. 3/2023.
- **Mampu Milik (affordable) industrial requirements**: Governed by prevailing state circular guidelines.
- **Groundwater monitoring wells/piezometers**: Requirement for pollution source monitoring stations.
- **Vehicle parking standards**: Minimum dimensions for car, motorcycle, OKU (disabled), EVCB, bus, lorry, and trailer parking bays, plus site circulation/access road requirements (illustrated in Rajah IND(5)).
- **Perimeter planting and open space standards**: Minimum setback/buffer widths for standalone and terrace/link industrial buildings, plus open space and recreation land requirements for industrial schemes (illustrated in Rajah IND(6) and IND(7)).
- **Prayer room (surau) requirements**: Sizing formulas based on worker count, ablution area dimensions.
- **Industrial scheme facilities**: Standards for TLK (car), TLM (motorcycle), OKU parking; ancillary structures allowed (electrical pylons, ATMs, guardhouses, water tanks, cooling towers, LPG tanks, septic tanks, etc.); mezzanine floor height/area limits; waste collection point siting rules.
- **Worker dormitories (Asrama Pekerja)**: Requirements under the Workers' Minimum Standards of Housing and Amenities Act 1990 (Act 446), including building setbacks, size limits (max 15% of plot GFA), and safety/segregation rules.
- **Supporting facilities**: Canteens, prayer/rest rooms, sports facilities, toilets, management offices, and staff transport considerations.
- **Buffer zone (Zon Penampan) requirements**: Minimum separation distances between light, medium, and heavy industry and residential areas (50m, 150m, 300m respectively), including permitted buffer-zone activities like green landscaping, drainage infrastructure, and utility corridors.

## IND1 INDUSTRI TERES DAN INDUSTRI TERES MAMPU MILIK (pages 30–32)

Summary: This section (IND1: Industri Teres dan Industri Teres Mampu Milik) from the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025 sets out planning guidelines and standards for Terrace Industrial and Affordable Terrace Industrial developments, covering: minimum lot size, service road/access road width, perimeter/side and rear lane requirements, front/rear setbacks (langkau api), planting perimeter, open space and recreation area, parking (TLK) for cars, motorcycles, lorries and OKU (disabled) parking, EV charging bay (EVCB) provisions, loading/unloading and goods storage areas, warehouse space, canteen/dining areas, prayer room (surau) and other support facilities (workers' dormitory, garbage disposal, subject to local authority requirements). It also includes illustrative diagrams (Rajah IND1(1)) showing front elevation, perspective, and side-view layouts of terrace industrial blocks, including maximum block length (91.44m/300ft), lot dimensions (6.10m x 24.38m/20ft x 80ft), road widths (20.12m/66ft), side/back lane widths (12.19m/40ft), and centralized parking layout.

## IND2 INDUSTRI SESEBUAH, INDUSTRI BERKEMBANG DAN INDUSTRI KLUSTER (pages 33–37)

Summary: This section (IND2: Industri Sesebuah, Industri Berkembang dan Industri Kluster) of the Selangor state planning guidelines (Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025) sets out detailed development standards for detached, semi-detached/developing, and clustered industrial building types across three lot size categories (small, medium, and cluster). It covers: minimum lot sizes and plinth ratio; minimum office space and building setbacks (front, side, rear); minimum road width; access/entry-exit requirements; perimeter planting; open space/recreation land; parking provisions for cars, call centres, motorcycles, OKU (disabled), lorries, and EV charging bays; loading/unloading bay dimensions for trailers, small lorries, and pending/holding areas; support facilities such as prayer rooms, worker dormitories, canteens, warehouses, and other amenities with their size/percentage requirements. It also includes illustrative site layout diagrams (Rajah IND2(1), (2), (3)) and 3D perspective renderings showing front and side elevations, setback dimensions, lot dimensions, and street/access configurations for each of the three industrial building types (Sesebuah/detached, Berkembang/developing, and Kluster/cluster).

## IND3 INDUSTRI STRATA BERTANAH (pages 38–40)

Summary: This section (IND3: Industri Strata Bertanah) from the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025" sets out planning standards for landed strata industrial developments. It covers: minimum lot sizes and dimensions for various industrial types (Industri Teres, Industri Sesebuah, Industri Teres Mampu Milik, Industri Berkembar, Industri Kluster); minimum lot coverage, front/side/rear setbacks, and rear lane requirements; building placement rules (e.g., office space limited to 30% of total gross floor area); access/ingress-outgress requirements (minimum access road width and lot access); planting buffer/perimeter landscaping requirements; parking (TLK), lorry parking (TLL), and motorcycle parking (TLM) provisions; electric vehicle charging bay (EVCB) requirements; OKU (disabled) parking; and supporting facility requirements such as canteens, prayer rooms, toilets, guardhouses, waste storage, and warehouse/loading areas. It includes illustrative cross-section diagrams (Rajah IND3(1)) showing setback dimensions for Teres, Sesebuah, Kluster, and Berkembar industrial building types, sourced from the GPPP Selangor Industrial Study.

## IND4 INDUSTRI STRATA BERTINGKAT (pages 41–43)

Summary: This section (IND4: Industri Strata Bertingkat / Stratified Multi-Storey Industrial) from the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Bahagian B) covers detailed planning guidelines and standards for stratified multi-storey industrial developments, including:

- **Permitted activities**: Subject to Local Plan/Structure Plan zoning
- **Site coverage standards**: Minimum lot area (1 acre), maximum plinth area (60%), maximum office floor area (30%)
- **Building setbacks**: Minimum front setback (12.19m/40'), side setback (7.62m/25'), and access road requirements (7.32m minimum opening)
- **EV charging infrastructure (EVCB)**: Requirements for developments over 50 TLK units, minimum 1% of parking bays, OKU (disabled) parking provisions
- **Parking and vehicle facilities**: Car park (TLK), lorry park (TLL), and motorcycle park (TLM) ratios based on floor area
- **Open space and setback landscaping**: 5% minimum site area for perimeter planting
- **Supporting facilities**: Office space, warehouse, recreational areas, prayer rooms, toilets, canteen/dining areas, loading/unloading zones, signboards, guard posts, flagpoles, and waste disposal areas
- **Loading bay dimensions**: Detailed specifications for lorry and trailer parking bays
- **Illustrative diagrams (Rajah IND4(1) and IND4(2))**: Cross-sectional/elevation views showing setback dimensions and a 3D perspective rendering of a stratified industrial building, illustrating how the standards translate into an actual building design with front/side/rear setbacks, plinth coverage, and road reserves.

## IND5 PUSAT DATA (pages 44–47)

Summary: This section (IND5: PUSAT DATA, pages 32–35) sets out Selangor's planning guidelines and standards for Data Centre developments, covering: site location criteria (accessibility, avoiding flood/landslide/erosion-prone and environmentally sensitive areas, distance from mineral/geological sites, airport-related restrictions); electrical power requirements by data centre scale (small, medium, large) including substation (SSU) voltage tiers (11kV, 33kV, 132kV, 275kV) with corresponding capacity (1–5 MVA up to >25 MVA) and minimum plot dimensions for each; generator/backup power and green technology provisions; water supply and internet/connectivity requirements (fibre optic, minimum 300 Mbps, redundancy); land use categorisation and plot ratio/building height limits tied to local plans and industrial land classifications; building design setbacks (front, side, rear building lines), road reserve, utility reserve, and corner cut requirements; parking provisions (car, motorcycle, OKU/disabled, loading bay); open space, perimeter planting, and buffer/setback zone requirements from surrounding land uses (e.g., residential areas); fire safety, universal design, and CCTV/security requirements; and data centre building components (office spaces, data hall, chiller/mantrap/battery rooms, generator room, pump room, guard house, toilets, auditorium/gym, etc.). It also includes illustrative diagrams (Rajah IND5(1) and IND5(2)) showing setback distances and buffer zone layout for a typical data centre building relative to adjacent roads and residential areas.

## IND6 PENGINAPAN PEKERJA BERPUSAT (pages 48–50)

Summary: This section (IND6: Penginapan Pekerja Berpusat / Centralised Labour Quarters – CLQ, Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025, pages 36–38) sets planning standards for centralised worker housing at industrial premises with ≥500 workers. It covers: (1) applicability and approval requirements (needed for SIA Kategori B, EIA review by JAS for light industries, and PBT zoning determination); (2) building setback/anjakan requirements (minimum building setback, setback to lot boundary, and inter-building spacing for standing/free buildings up to 3 storeys and above 3 storeys, given both as "between building and boundary" and "between buildings" dimensions); (3) road circulation requirements (dedicated entry/exit access, additional access for emergencies, and provision of pick-up/drop-off lay-by zones); (4) perimeter zone/greenery requirements (perimeter planting width, buffer/setback requirements, and landscaping for visual screening); (5) parking requirements for cars, motorcycles, bicycles and buses, set as percentages of resident/staff/visitor population; (6) required and "best practice" facilities to be provided, spanning basic shared amenities (prayer room, kitchen/dining, laundry, sick bay/quarantine room, management office), safety facilities (guard house, security fencing, CCTV, biometric access, fire safety per JBPM requirements, separate male/female dormitory blocks), recreational facilities (futsal, badminton, sepak takraw, volleyball courts, recreation room) per the Housing and Centralised Labour Quarters Standards Regulations 2020, and optional convenience facilities (mini-mart, barber, clinic, ATM); and (7) an illustrative site layout diagram (Rajah IND6(1)) showing a model centralised worker dormitory layout with separate male/female hostels, guard house, drop-off zone, water tank, septic tank, management office, prayer hall, dining hall, sports court, innovation/counselling centre, retail kiosks, and parking area arranged along local roads.

## IND7 MANAGED INDUSTRIAL PARK (MIP) (pages 51–58)

Summary: This section (IND7: Managed Industrial Park (MIP)) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Bahagian B) sets out planning guidelines for Managed Industrial Parks jointly developed with the Selangor State Planning and Local Government Committee. It specifies a minimum area of 200 acres or as required, and details design controls for security guard posts (non-strata and strata), including minimum dimensions (1.83m x 2.44m, or up to 6' x 8'), positioning at least 6.10m from the main road/road shoulder, height restrictions not exceeding the security fence height (max 4.57m/15ft), and requirements that they not obstruct traffic sightlines or road reserves. It also covers procedures for interim/temporary guard post structures requiring District Land Office (PTD) or Temporary Occupation Licence (LPS) approval under the National Land Code 1965, and requirements for relocating temporary guard posts and obtaining local authority (PBT) building/temporary permits. Further guidelines address design controls (roof integration with main building, utility concealment, TNB substation and refuse chamber facade harmonization), security controls (Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design/CPTED principles, boom gates, CCTV at guard posts, 24-hour monitoring), and the Industrial Service Centre (PPI), which includes facilities such as a waste management/collection centre, information and data management centre (Taman Perindustrian Management Office/Command Center), central vehicle parking, food court, prayer room/surau, public toilets, motorcycle/car parking, and a lorry/trailer/depot area — illustrated with two design renderings (Rajah IND7(1) and IND7(2)) showing the guard post and PPI layout. The remaining pages (53–58) are blank or divider pages, ending with a Jabatan Perancangan Bandar dan Desa Negeri Selangor (PLAN Malaysia Selangor) back cover.
