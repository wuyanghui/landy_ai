# TUJUAN (pages 14–14)

Summary: This page (page 2, Bahagian A) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor, 2025" document covers two main sections:

**1.0 Tujuan (Objectives)** – lists four purposes of this planning guideline: (1) to assist PBT, agencies, and private sector in planning, controlling, and processing all types of business development applications; (2) to serve as a reference to ease and standardize the approval process; (3) to ensure business development infrastructure meets planning principles; and (4) noting that this guideline updates and replaces the previous version, referencing the Manual Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Negeri Selangor Edisi Keempat, 2022.

**2.0 Latar Belakang (Background), Section 2.1 Isu dan Cabaran dalam Pembangunan Perniagaan (Issues and Challenges in Business Development)** – explains that business activities are now planned comprehensively as they are a major component of land-use change and contribute to economic growth and new business perspectives. It notes that business developments are no longer standalone but integrated with other supporting activities, with growth seen in large-scale/complex developments such as hypermarkets or malls. It stresses that special attention must be given to business planning aspects for orderly, appropriate development that follows and meets customer needs. The section then lists five specific problems commonly encountered in planning and business development: (1) traffic congestion disrupting access to business areas; (2) insufficient parking space; (3) inadequate building height for business needs; (4) lack of green/buffer zones in controlled business areas; and (5) low visual/aesthetic quality and public transport connectivity issues in business areas.

# DASAR DAN POLISI PEMBANGUNAN (pages 15–20)

Summary: This section of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Bahagian A) outlines the policy and planning foundation guiding business/commercial development in Selangor, covering six key frameworks:

1. **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** — 7 relevant SDGs (Affordable & Clean Energy, Decent Work & Economic Growth, Industry/Innovation/Infrastructure, Sustainable Cities & Communities, Responsible Consumption & Production, Climate Action, Partnerships for the Goals) linked to business planning.

2. **Rancangan Malaysia Ke-12 (RMK-12, 2021–2025)** — national development plan focus on smart city development (smart tech, open data, IoT), infrastructure upgrades (roads, utilities, digitalization), support for SMEs (financing, access, skills training), and services sector development.

3. **Rancangan Fizikal Negara Ke-4 (RFN-4)** — national physical plan with three teras (thrusts): strategic dynamic growth, spatial resilience/climate change adaptation, and a conducive/livable environment, including digital infrastructure and logistics.

4. **Dasar Perbandaran Negara Kedua (DPN2, 2016–2025)** — second national urbanization policy with objectives and strategies for improving urban business competitiveness, infrastructure, local economic growth, efficient business planning, logistics systems, market data, and innovation-friendly environments.

5. **Rancangan Struktur Negeri (RSN) Selangor 2035** — state structural plan draft with three teras: dynamic/competitive state, sustainable/resilient state (green infrastructure), and resource-efficient/livable state (housing, social wellbeing, economic balance).

6. **Dasar Perancangan Pembangunan Berorientasikan Transit (TOD) Negeri Selangor** — transit-oriented development policy (from 2016, gazetted 2016) with nine core principles covering mixed-use development, density, affordable housing/commercial units, pedestrian/cycling paths, parking, feeder bus services, and green building promotion within transit catchment zones.

# HENTIAN REHAT DAN PUSAT RAWAT (RnR) (pages 53–54)

Summary: This partial document (pages 41–42, "Bahagian B" of the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor 2025) presents **PN10: Hentian Rehat dan Rawat (RnR)** — planning standards for rest and service areas — covering:

- **Building dimensions/coverage**: minimum lot coverage (open/enclosed stalls), ground floor area, building height (4 storeys), road access, and perimeter planting requirements based on lot size.
- **Parking and vehicle facilities**: minimum motorcycle and car parking provision (per unit floor area), EV charging bay (EVCB) requirements including OKU-accessible EVCB, and disabled (OKU) parking bay quotas with ramps/railing.
- **Accessibility and welfare facilities**: OKU facilities, prayer room (surau) sizing and orientation, fire safety/firemen access per MS1184, and provisions for OKU (persons with disabilities) in Bomba/fire-fighting design.
- **Landscape and recreation**: minimum landscaped area percentage, indoor/rooftop garden allowances, and courtyard/plaza requirements.
