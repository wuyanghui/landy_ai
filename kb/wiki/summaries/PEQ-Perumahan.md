# Preface (pages 1–12)

Summary: This partial document is the front matter of "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Selangor State Housing Planning Guidelines and Standards 2025), published by Jabatan Perancangan Bandar dan Desa Negeri Selangor (PLANMalaysia Selangor). It includes: the cover page; copyright/ownership notice (Hak Cipta) confirming the document is protected and citing its approval at MMKN meetings (Ke-33/2025 on 7 Nov 2025 and Ke-34/2025 on 12 Nov 2025) with publisher contact details; a joint preparation credits page listing PLANMalaysia Selangor together with 12 local authorities across Selangor (e.g., MBSA, MBPJ, MBSJ, MBDK, MPS, MPAJ, MPKj, MPSepang, MPKL, MPKS, MPHS, MDSB); a "Klausa Kecualian" (saving clause) and "Penafian" (disclaimer) regarding use of illustrations and superseded documents; a Prakata (foreword) from the Director of the department explaining this edition supersedes the 2022 Manual Garis Panduan Perancangan Perumahan (approved MMKN Ke-15/2022 and Ke-16/2022), summarizing its aims (updating housing policy, sustainability, smart technology, inclusive/Selangor Smart and Caring approach), and describing the review process involving professional bodies (LPBM, PAM, IEM, MIP, REHDA) and 12 local authorities; a detailed Table of Contents (Kandungan) covering Bahagian A (Scope, Objectives, Background, Policy, Definitions, Best Practices, Selangorku Initiative, General Standards) and Bahagian B (Special Planning Guidelines for terrace, cluster, strata, semi-D, bungalow, Selangorku, low-cost, and apartment/condominium housing types) with corresponding List of Figures; a List of Schedules and List of Abbreviations (e.g., CBD, CCTV, EVCB, LRT, MMKN, MRT, OKU, RSKU, TOD, TNB); a Glossary (Glosari) defining key planning terms (e.g., Anjakan Bangunan, Densiti, Nisbah Plot, Perimeter Planting, Rizab Sungai, Skim Strata, Zon Pengaruh Transit); and a final "Punca Kuasa/Peruntukan Perundangan" (Legal Basis) section listing 17 governing acts, policies, and reference plans (e.g., Akta 172, DPN2, TOD policies, PLANMalaysia guidelines, RFN-4, RMK-12).

# TUJUAN (pages 14–14)

Summary: This page (rotated 90°, page 2 of Bahagian A) presents Section 1.0 "Tujuan" (Objectives) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025" (GPPP), outlining four objectives: it replaces the previous 2022 GPPP manual, assists PBT and agencies in planning/controlling housing development, guides uniform physical planning standards for developers and authorities, and aims to ensure adequate infrastructure is planned alongside housing. It also begins Section 2.0 "Latar Belakang" (Background), including 2.1 "Isu dan Cabaran dalam Pembangunan Perumahan" (Issues and Challenges in Housing Development), listing six key challenges: outdated/inflexible planning trends, conventional and unattractive housing design, road/traffic systems not meeting safety and quality needs, inadequate consideration of infrastructure elements, insufficient and poorly located open/green spaces, and lack of consideration for OKU (disabled), the elderly, and children in planning. The page emphasizes the goal of providing housing that is conducive, comfortable, quality, safe, and harmonious, and hints at further discussion of planning-stage problems and mechanisms in the following section (partially cut off at page bottom, referencing Section 3.0/3.1).

# DASAR DAN POLISI PEMBANGUNAN (pages 15–23)

Summary: This section of the document (Bahagian A, pages 3–11 of *Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025*) covers the policy and planning foundation for Selangor's housing framework:

1. **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** – 7 relevant SDGs (No Poverty, Good Health, Clean Water & Sanitation, Affordable Clean Energy, Industry/Innovation/Infrastructure, Sustainable Cities, Climate Action) and their housing-related targets.

2. **National and state policy alignment** – summarizes how the state housing guideline connects to:
   - **RMK12** (12th Malaysia Plan, 2021–2025) — 5 housing sector focus areas (supply, financing access, governance, inclusive housing, sustainable urban development).
   - **RFN4** (4th National Physical Plan) — spatial/climate resilience, land use, and livable-neighborhood teras.
   - **DPN2** (2nd National Housing Policy) — objectives for adequate, quality, affordable home ownership and rental housing, with associated strategies/targets (e.g. TOD housing, Rumah Mampu Milik, multi-generation housing).
   - **DRN 2018–2025** (National Housing Policy) — 5 strategic thrusts for quality, accessible, sustainable, coordinated, and institutionally strong housing.
   - **RSN Selangor 2035** (State Structure Plan) — 3 Teras Wilayah Bandar Makmur and 5 Hala Tuju Strategik (MM1–MM5) on housing type/cost, ownership, rental, green tech, and safety.

3. **Bandar Berdaya Huni Negeri Selangor** – 4 pillars (Growth, Livability, Sustainability, Resiliency) guiding livable city development.

4. **Dasar Perancangan Pembangunan Berorientasikan Transit (TOD) Selangor** – 9 core principles for transit-oriented, mixed-use, walkable, densified development around transit stations (400m radius).

5. **Dasar Perumahan Mampu Milik Negeri Selangor (Rumah Selangorku 3.0)** – zoning framework dividing the state into 4 zones (Zon 1: high-density urban, Zon 2: urban, Zon 3: urban fringe, Zon 4: rural), with a district/mukim breakdown table and a state map (Rajah PR(1)) showing zone boundaries.

6. **Peratusan Penyediaan Rumah Mampu Milik (Jadual PR(2))** – required percentage quotas for affordable housing provision by zone and land size (from <5 acres up to >50 acres), differentiated for TOD/TAD/CBD areas vs. non-TOD areas, and by housing type (Solo/Duo/Trio/Quad, landed vs. strata).

7. **Komponen Pembangunan (Jadual PR(3))** – specifications for Rumah Selangorku 3.0 housing types (Solo, Duo, Trio, Quad/Teres), including minimum built-up area, number of bedrooms, and fixed selling prices (RM114,750–RM250,000), plus illustrative floor plans and building renders.

# AMALAN TERBAIK DAN INISIATIF PEMBANGUNAN PERUMAHAN (pages 24–24)

Summary: This page (p.12 of the document, "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor") introduces Section 4.0 "Amalan Terbaik dan Inisiatif Pembangunan" (Best Practices and Development Initiatives), describing six key housing development initiatives adopted in Selangor amid Malaysia's economic and property sector growth: (1) Perumahan Mampu Milik (Affordable Housing) — providing homes matching residents' purchasing power; (2) Reka Bentuk Inklusif untuk Pelbagai Generasi (Inclusive Multigenerational Housing) — units accommodating young families, adults, and the elderly; (3) Pembangunan Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) — integrating housing with LRT/MRT and pedestrian/cycling access; (4) Pengurusan Sisa Mampan (Sustainable Waste Management) — recycling centers and composite elements for easier waste collection; (5) Infrastruktur Pintar dan Teknologi Hijau (Smart Infrastructure and Green Technology) — smart tech, safety sensors, solar panels, and rainwater harvesting; and (6) Pembangunan Bertingkat dan Mampu Milik (Affordable High-Rise Development) — multi-story housing meeting community needs and improving quality of life. It includes illustrative figures: Rajah PR(4) showing solar panel roofing for energy conservation, Rajah PR(5) showing an exclusive multigenerational home interior, and Rajah PR(6) showing affordable high-rise residential towers.

# PRINSIP ASAS PERANCANGAN DAN DEFINISI PERUMAHAN (pages 25–26)

Summary: This section (pages 13–14 of "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025," Bahagian A) covers Section 5.0, "Prinsip Asas Perancangan dan Definisi Perumahan." It opens with Section 5.1, six guiding design principles for housing planning: (1) offering sufficient, affordable, tenure-appropriate housing types; (2) self-contained, well-integrated development complete with strategic, safe locations; (3) creating comfortable, conducive, safe living environments matched to user needs; (4) preserving natural surroundings, topography, and ecology; (5) fostering a culture of cleanliness and beauty in harmony with the community; and (6) encouraging social cohesion and positive community interaction. Section 5.2 then defines common housing types with accompanying aerial/street photos: Rumah Teres (terrace house, ≥3 units sharing walls), Rumah Berkembar (semi-detached, 2 units sharing one wall), Rumah Kluster (cluster housing, 4 units sharing walls in a small group), Rumah Bandar (town house, ≤4 storeys with strata title), Rumah Sesebuah (detached/bungalow, standalone with no shared walls), Rumah Kluster Berkembar (an adapted cluster concept providing 8 units per plot with shared communal/interaction spaces, including for the elderly and children), Pangsapuri/Kondominium (apartment/condominium, multi-storey strata buildings with shared facilities like surau, recreational areas, and security), and Perumahan Strata (strata housing schemes under the Strata Titles Act 1985 [Act 318], covering parcels, common property, and management arrangements, with examples including apartments, condominiums, and gated-and-guarded landed communities).

# RUMAH TERES (pages 32–33)

Summary: This section (PR1: Rumah Teres) from Selangor's Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan 2025 sets out planning standards for terrace houses, including a detailed specification table covering minimum lot size (6.10m x 18.29m/800 sq ft), minimum road reserve, maximum building height (3 storeys/55 ft), minimum room count (3 bedrooms), maximum plot ratio, maximum floor-to-floor height, maximum block length (97.54m), minimum service road, minimum cul-de-sac road length, minimum front/side/corner/rear setbacks, minimum corner cutting, minimum fire break between terrace blocks, and minimum radius — each paired with reference figures in both metric and imperial units. It also lists minimum open space and recreation area requirements (10% for developments ≥5 acres or ≥50 units, 5% for smaller schemes), with notes citing the relevant Selangor planning circulars/guidelines governing conversion and additions to terrace houses. The section includes site layout diagrams (Rajah PR1(1) showing building setbacks in plan view, Rajah PR1(2) showing cross-sectional/side elevation setbacks) and an illustrative 3D rendering (Rajah PR1(3)) of a terrace house row.

# RUMAH BERKEMBAR DAN RUMAH KLUSTER (pages 34–39)

Summary: This section (PR2: Rumah Berkembar dan Rumah Kluster) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025" sets out planning standards for semi-detached (Rumah Berkembar) and cluster houses (Rumah Kluster) in Selangor. It covers minimum/maximum criteria including lot size, minimum floor area, unit/room/storey counts, building height, plot ratio, corner-lot setbacks (front, side, rear, and corner-cut), cul-de-sac road dimensions, service road width, pedestrian walkway provision, and minimum open space/recreation requirements (10% generally, 5% for developments under 50 units or 5 acres). It includes annotated site layout diagrams (Rajah PR2(1), PR2(4), PR2(8)) showing setback dimensions for a row of terrace/cluster lots with corner-cut allowances; cross-section diagrams (Rajah PR2(2), PR2(5), PR2(9)) illustrating front, rear, and side setbacks relative to the service road and building envelope; and 3D illustrative renderings (Rajah PR2(3), PR2(6), PR2(7), PR2(10), PR2(11)) showing front, rear, and aerial perspective views of built semi-detached and cluster house developments along a street.

# RUMAH SESEBUAH (pages 40–41)

Summary: This partial document (pages 28–29) presents "PR3: RUMAH SESEBUAH" (detached house) standards from the Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025 (Bahagian B). It includes a detailed specification table covering minimum lot size (15.24m x 22.86m / 1,350 sq ft), minimum floor area (125.42 sq m), maximum building levels (3 storeys), minimum room count (3 rooms), maximum building/floor height (16.76m / 4.27m to floor), minimum service road width (15.24m), minimum cul-de-sac dimensions, minimum building setbacks (front, side, rear, corner lot, and porch/car canopy setbacks), minimum corner cut, minimum building frontage, and open space/recreation land requirements (10% for schemes of 50+ units/5+ acres, 5% for smaller schemes, with the higher applicable requirement used). A note clarifies that strata-titled detached houses follow separate strata housing guidelines while landed detached houses must comply with these PR3 and PR4 guidelines plus additional requirements. The pages also include diagrams: Rajah PR3(1) illustrating building setback dimensions on a lot plan, Rajah PR3(2) an aerial illustration of a detached house scheme, Rajah PR3(3) a cross-section view showing side setbacks between houses, and Rajah PR3(4) a front-view illustration of detached houses, all sourced from the Kajian GPPP Negeri Selangor (Perumahan).

# PERUMAHAN STRATA (pages 42–46)

Summary: This section (PR4: Perumahan Strata Bertanah) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Bahagian B, pages 30–34) sets out planning guidelines and standards for landed strata housing development. It covers: minimum/maximum site and unit specifications (site size, number of units, lot size, plot ratio, building height/storeys, floor area); road and access requirements (internal roads, cul-de-sacs, service roads, rear lanes, side lanes, corner cuts, and setback/building line distances for front, side, rear, and corner lots); parking and disabled-accessible (OKU) parking provisions; fencing/wall and vehicle entry-exit requirements; open space and recreational area provisions (including perimeter planting and landscaping ratios based on unit count); communal facility provisions such as kindergartens/nurseries (Tadika/Taska), prayer rooms/surau, community halls (dewan), management offices, and guard posts, with sizing formulas tied to unit counts; notes on strata subdivision applicability for landed housing not exceeding 4 storeys and compliance with related guidelines (KM2-4-GP, OKU standards); and illustrative diagrams (Rajah PR4(1)–PR4(5)) showing building setbacks/anjakan, cross-sections of strata landed housing, front elevation views, and an aerial illustration of a mixed strata housing scheme combining landed and high-rise residential development with shared facilities like guard posts, fencing, open space, and public amenities.

# RUMAH SELANGORKU (RUMAH TERES, RUMAH BANDAR DAN PANGSAPURI) (pages 58–66)

Summary: This section (pages 46–51, plus blank/back-cover pages) of the "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025" (Selangor State Housing Planning Guidelines and Standards) covers **PR7: Rumah Selangorku (Rumah Teres dan Rumah Bandar serta Pangsapuri)** — planning standards for affordable "Rumah Selangorku" housing schemes, including:

- **Terrace and strata landed housing (Rumah Teres/Rumah Bandar)**: minimum lot size, floor area, bilangan bilik, storey height/count, floor-to-floor height, unit ownership, block length, cul-de-sac requirements, back lane/fire break/side lane dimensions, corner-cut, building setbacks (front/rear/side/end lot), internal roads, and minimum open space/recreation land and perimeter planting requirements, with reference illustrations (Rajah PR7(1) and PR7(2)) of built examples.
- **Apartment/flat housing (Pangsapuri)**: site size, plinth area, density, plot coverage limits, floor area ratios, building setbacks from roads/boundaries, podium levels, internal road/access requirements, lift and bedroom-count standards, provisions for OKU (disabled), elderly, and vulnerable groups, with illustrations (Rajah PR7(3) and PR7(4)) showing podium and accessibility design.
- **Parking standards**: car, motorcycle, quad-bike, and OKU parking ratios (including tandem parking rules), plus EV charging bay (EVCB) provision requirements.
- **Communal and social facilities**: prayer rooms/surau, funeral management space, non-Muslim worship space, halls, mailboxes, management office, elderly activity spaces (with unit-count-based sizing tables), childcare (Tadika/Taska), waste collection points, and other suggested amenities (kiosks, laundry rooms, package lockers, green building concepts), plus siting/location principles emphasizing safety, accessibility, and centrality — illustrated with an aerial view (Rajah PR7(5)) of a sample Rumah Selangorku Pangsapuri development.
- The final pages are blank and the back cover, showing the issuing agency: Jabatan Perancangan Bandar dan Desa Negeri Selangor (PLAN Malaysia Selangor).
