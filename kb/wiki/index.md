# Knowledge Base Index

## Documents
- [[summaries/PEQ-alam-sekitar]] (pageindex) — Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Alam Sekitar Negeri Selangor 2025 — Selangor state government's environmental planning guidelines and standards, published by PLANMalaysia Selangor, covering scope and development policy (Bahagian A) and special guidelines for hillside/highland areas, peat soil, lakes, tree preservation, coastal zones, river reserves, and rainwater harvesting/water efficiency systems (Bahagian B).
- [[summaries/PEQ-K-Masyarakat]] (pageindex) — This document is the 2025 "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor," a state guideline issued by PLANMalaysia Selangor establishing planning policies and detailed site/design standards (KM1–KM9) for community facilities—including health, education, safety, religious, burial, community, library, open space/recreation, market, and public transport terminal facilities—across Selangor.
- [[summaries/PEQ-infrastruktur]] (pageindex) — "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor 2025," a PLANMalaysia Selangor guideline document establishing infrastructure planning policies and technical standards across road hierarchy, parking (cars, motorcycles, buses, lorries, bicycles, OKU, women, EV charging bays), electrical substations and TNB transmission corridors, telecommunications towers, utility corridors, and sewerage treatment plants for the 12 local authorities of Selangor state.
- [[summaries/PEQ-Perniagaan]] (pageindex) — "Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perniagaan Negeri Selangor 2025," published by PLANMalaysia Selangor, is a comprehensive planning standards manual detailing policy frameworks, general design principles, and specific technical requirements (site coverage, setbacks, parking, EV charging, OKU accessibility, and support facilities) for twenty categories of commercial and business developments in Selangor, ranging from shophouses and hypermarkets to hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, golf courses, and sports complexes.
- [[summaries/PEQ-Perumahan]] (pageindex) — Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perumahan Negeri Selangor, 2025 — PLANMalaysia Selangor's official state housing planning guidelines and standards, covering policy background, general standards, and specific planning requirements for terrace, semi-detached, cluster, detached, strata, condominium, and Rumah Selangorku affordable housing types.
- [[summaries/PEQ-Perindustrian]] (pageindex) — Garis Panduan dan Piawaian Perancangan Perindustrian Negeri Selangor 2025 is PLANMalaysia Selangor's state industrial planning guideline that establishes development policy, general planning standards, and type-specific design requirements (terrace, detached/cluster, strata, stratified, data centres, worker housing, and Managed Industrial Parks) for industrial land use across Selangor.

## Concepts
- [[concepts/rainwater-harvesting-spah]] — Selangor's mandatory rainwater harvesting (SPAH) design standard for buildings with large roof catchment areas.
- [[concepts/river-reserve-development]] — Selangor's zoning, setback, and structural standards governing development within and alongside river reserves.
- [[concepts/kawasan-sensitif-alam-sekitar-ksas]] — Environmentally Sensitive Areas classification system governing hillside, lake, coastal, river, and tree-protected zones in Selangor state planning.
- [[concepts/burial-and-cemetery-planning-standards]] — Standards for religious and multi-faith cemetery siting, grave layout, tiered burial, crematoria, and columbaria in Selangor.
- [[concepts/vertical-facility-development]] — Compact multi-storey design approach for community facilities on land-constrained urban sites in Selangor.
- [[concepts/population-based-facility-hierarchy]] — Tiered system scaling site area, capacity, and infrastructure to population catchment across all Selangor community facility standards.
- [[concepts/cpted-crime-prevention-through-environmental-design]] — A design-based crime-prevention framework (natural surveillance, access control, territoriality, maintenance) applied across Selangor's community facility standards.
- [[concepts/geological-site-clearance-bpgt]] — BPGT is a JMG-issued geological suitability clearance required before siting community facilities on sensitive or unstable land.
- [[concepts/electrical-substation-siting]] — Siting, sizing, and setback standards for TNB electrical substations and transmission corridors in Selangor.
- [[concepts/utility-corridor-standards]] — Planning standards for shared utility corridors (electrical, communications, water, sewerage, gas) placed within and alongside road reserves in Selangor.
- [[concepts/road-hierarchy-selangor]] — Selangor's road classification system (rural R1–R6, urban U1–U6) that sets design speed, reserve width, and access-control standards underlying site access requirements for infrastructure and community facilities.
- [[concepts/parking-standards]] — Shared framework of vehicle parking bay categories, ratios, and design standards across Selangor's commercial, infrastructure, and community facility guidelines.
- [[concepts/ev-charging-bay-requirements]] — Selangor's mandatory EV charging bay quotas, siting, and fire-safety standards spanning commercial, infrastructure, and community facility guidelines.
- [[concepts/oku-accessibility-standards]] — Mandatory OKU (disabled) accessibility requirements — parking, ramps, signage, and design standards — recurring across all Selangor planning guidelines.
- [[concepts/inclusive-accessible-housing-design]] — Cross-typology planning standards ensuring housing accommodates OKU, elderly, and children's needs.
- [[concepts/affordable-housing-provision-standards]] — Selangor's zoning-based quotas, pricing, and unit standards mandating affordable housing provision in every development.
- [[concepts/transit-oriented-development]] — State policy concentrating density and mixed use around transit stations, shaping housing, commercial, and community facility siting.
- [[concepts/worker-welfare-facilities]] — Statutory housing, amenity, and safety standards required for industrial workers under Selangor's 2025 planning guidelines.
- [[concepts/buffer-zone-requirements]] — Mandatory separation distances protecting residential and sensitive land uses from industrial, infrastructure, and community-facility hazards.
- [[concepts/sustainable-industrial-planning]] — Policy-driven approach embedding environmental, energy, and design practices into industrial development to reduce ecological impact.
